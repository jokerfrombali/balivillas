The area, honestly

Living in Sanur for a month or more

Sanur is the part of Bali people choose when they are staying rather than visiting. It is residential first and touristic second: quiet lanes, low buildings, no club strip, and an eight-kilometre beachside promenade that is flat, shaded and closed to cars — the reason so many long-stay residents here walk and cycle instead of renting a scooter.

The practical side is what keeps people for a second and third month. Supermarkets, pharmacies, clinics and the island’s main hospitals are minutes away, there are international schools and coworking spaces, and Grab and Gojek cover the whole area properly. The airport is 30–45 minutes by car, and the fast-boat terminal to Nusa Penida and Lembongan is walking distance from the north end of the beach.

Compared with Canggu, Sanur trades nightlife and surf for calm, space and a shorter commute to anything you actually need. It suits remote workers who want to get work done, families settling in for a school term, couples on a long winter escape and retirees — which is exactly who rents our villas here by the month.

Which villas work best for a long stay in Sanur

  • 1–2 bedrooms for solo remote workers and couples: the most-asked configuration and the one that books furthest ahead
  • 3 bedrooms for families staying a school term, with room to work and eat properly rather than just sleep
  • 4 bedrooms and up for two families sharing or for a group that wants one base for several months

Questions

Monthly rentals in Sanur: your questions

How much is a monthly villa rental in Sanur?
It depends on the size of the villa and the season. Rates for each of our Sanur villas are shown on the cards above; where a villa shows “rate on request”, message us and we will send the figure the same day. Ours are fully furnished, serviced villas with housekeeping and pool care included, so they sit above a bare yearly lease from an agent and well below the nightly rate multiplied by thirty.
What is the minimum stay?
One calendar month. Some villas take 21 nights in low season — ask, and we will tell you which ones are open.
Is electricity included?
No. Electricity is metered and billed at cost at the end of each month, which is the norm in Bali; air conditioning is what moves the figure. Everything else — housekeeping, pool and garden care, Wi-Fi, maintenance and call-outs — is in the rate.
Can I extend month by month?
Yes, subject to the calendar. Tell us early and we will hold the villa; long stays are priced better the further ahead you commit.
Do you take a deposit?
A refundable security deposit, returned after check-out once the villa and the electricity meter are checked. There is no agency commission on top — you are renting from the manager, not through a broker.
