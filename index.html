Who we are

Bali locals + AI.
The smartest way to find your villa.

BaliVillas.ai was built in Bali, by people who live here. We combined an AI-powered search engine with something no algorithm can fake — a local team that has personally visited, verified, and handpicked every one of our 500+ villas across Canggu, Uluwatu, Ubud, Seminyak, Sanur and beyond.

We started with a simple frustration: finding a villa in Bali meant hours of scrolling, outdated listings, and photos that never matched reality. So we built a platform where you describe your perfect stay in plain words — and get real, verified matches in seconds.

But we're more than search. Behind the platform is a professional hospitality team managing villas on the ground: daily housekeeping, pool and garden care, guest support that actually answers. That's why owners trust us with their properties — and why guests come back.
