Yes, Sanur is good for families visiting Bali. The beach is calm and shallow thanks to an offshore reef, the beachfront path is flat and safe for walking or cycling with children, and the area has a genuinely quiet, low-traffic feel compared with Canggu or Seminyak. It is one of the few parts of south Bali where you can walk to the beach, a café and a pharmacy without crossing a busy road.

We manage 11 villas in Sanur and see a steady mix of families booking directly with us, so the points below come from what those guests actually ask and need.

## Why the water matters more than people expect

Bali's west and south coasts have real surf, which is exciting for adults but a genuine hazard for small children. Sanur sits behind a reef that breaks the swell before it reaches the shore, so the water near the beach is calm and shallow for a good stretch out — closer to a large, gentle lagoon than open ocean. That single difference is why so many families end up choosing Sanur over Canggu or Uluwatu.

## What else makes a base family-friendly

- A flat, paved beachfront path for walking, jogging or renting bicycles with a child seat
- Villas with private, fenced pools rather than shared or unfenced ones
- Short, manageable drives to clinics, pharmacies and supermarkets
- Quiet streets away from nightlife noise at bedtime
- Restaurants used to children, with high chairs and simple menus

Nusa Dua is a reasonable alternative for families, built around large resort compounds with calm water, though it has a more manufactured, resort-only feel and less to walk to outside the hotel grounds than Sanur's town centre. Canggu can work for families too, but the surf and traffic mean more careful planning around where exactly you stay.

## What to check on any villa if you are travelling with children

- Is the pool fenced, or does it open directly onto a living area?
- Is there a second bedroom or a layout that lets children sleep separately once settled?
- How far is the nearest clinic or pharmacy, and is transport arranged or needed?
- Is the kitchen equipped enough to prepare simple food, if that matters for younger children?

Our [villas in Sanur](/villas/sanur/) include several family-sized options, such as a 4-bedroom villa with a shallow kids' pool area and a garden villa near Mertasari Beach — details like a shallow end are worth asking about specifically, since not every villa pool has one.

## Schools, longer stays and settling in

Families staying for a month or longer sometimes ask about local international schools and short-term enrolment options in the wider Denpasar and Sanur area; this is worth researching directly with the school in question rather than assuming availability, since places and short-stay policies vary year to year. For shorter holidays, the more relevant planning point is usually just pacing the days sensibly around nap times and the midday heat, which Sanur's calm, low-stimulation environment makes easier than a livelier area would.

## Packing and preparing for a family stay

A few practical additions make a real difference to a family week in Sanur: reef-safe sunscreen, a wide-brimmed hat for each child, a basic first-aid kit, and insect repellent suited to young skin. Most villas, including ours, provide basic kitchen equipment, so travel bottles, a portable high chair or a travel cot are worth checking with your host rather than assuming they are included, since this varies by property.

## Evenings in Sanur with children

Sanur is quiet after dark by Bali standards, which is a genuine advantage for families managing bedtimes, but it also means less to do in the evening within walking distance than Canggu or Seminyak. Most families find this trade works in their favour on a longer stay, though it is worth knowing before you book if you are hoping for evening entertainment nearby. ## FAQ

### Is Bali generally safe and good for families?
Bali as a whole works well for families, but conditions vary sharply by area — calm-water beach towns like Sanur or resort areas like Nusa Dua suit young children far better than surf-focused areas like Canggu or Uluwatu, where currents and traffic need more careful management.

### Is Sanur or Nusa Dua better for a family with toddlers?
Both offer calm water and quieter streets. Sanur has a more walkable town centre with independent villas and restaurants; Nusa Dua is more resort-based with less to walk to outside the compounds. Families who want to explore on foot tend to prefer Sanur.

### Can you swim safely at Sanur beach with young children?
Yes, in most conditions — the reef keeps the water calm close to shore. Always check tide and current conditions locally and supervise children directly, as with any beach.

### Is Canggu good for families with kids?
It can work, particularly in the calmer Berawa or Pererenan pockets, but the surf and heavier traffic mean more planning is needed than in Sanur, and pool fencing varies more by villa.

### What age group does Sanur suit best?
Sanur works well across ages — young children benefit from the calm water, older children have space to cycle the beachfront path, and it also suits multi-generational trips with grandparents who prefer a quieter, flatter base.
