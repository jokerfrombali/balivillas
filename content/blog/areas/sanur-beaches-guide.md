Sanur beach is not a single beach but a roughly five-kilometre stretch of shoreline on Bali's east coast, running from the harbour area in the north down past Sindhu, Segara and Mertasari in the south, connected by a flat, paved beachfront path. An offshore reef keeps the water calm, which is the defining feature that separates Sanur from the surf beaches on the west and south coasts.

We manage 11 villas in Sanur, several within a short walk of this beachfront path, so this guide covers what we point guests toward depending on what they want from the beach.

## The main stretches, from north to south

| Stretch | Character |
|---|---|
| Sanur Harbour area | Boats to Nusa Lembongan and Nusa Penida, busier with day-trippers |
| Sindhu Beach | Popular local spot, food stalls nearby, lively mornings |
| Sanur (Segara) Beach | Central stretch, hotels and cafés along the path |
| Mertasari Beach | Quieter, wider sand at low tide, popular with kite-surfers when windy |

Most of the path between these stretches is walkable or cyclable in well under an hour end to end, which makes it easy to explore more than one on foot during a stay.

## Why the water is calm here

A coral reef sits a few hundred metres offshore along most of Sanur's coastline, which breaks up incoming swell before it reaches the beach. That is why Sanur has a reputation as a calm, family-friendly beach rather than a surf destination — the trade-off is that serious surfers should look toward Canggu, Uluwatu or Kuta instead.

## What the beachfront path offers

The paved path running the length of Sanur's beach is one of its best features: shaded in sections, dotted with cafés, and genuinely usable for walking, jogging or cycling rather than just a decorative promenade. It is one of the few places in south Bali where a car or scooter is not the default way to get around for several kilometres at a stretch.

- Rent a bicycle for a half-day and cover the full path, stopping at cafés along the way
- Walk it in the early morning before the heat builds, which is when it is busiest with locals exercising
- Use it as your main route between villa, beach and restaurants rather than driving short distances

## Boat trips from Sanur harbour

Sanur's harbour is the main departure point for fast boats to Nusa Lembongan, Nusa Ceningan and Nusa Penida, making it a practical base if a Nusa Islands day trip or overnight is part of your plans. Boats typically run in the morning; book ahead in high season as popular departures fill up.

## Tides and the best time to swim

Sanur's beach changes noticeably with the tide — at low tide, wide sandbanks and shallow reef flats are exposed, which can leave very little water to actually swim in at some points along the shore, while high tide brings the water closer to the beachfront path and generally offers better swimming conditions. Checking a tide chart for the day, easily available online or from most villa hosts, makes a real difference to whether an afternoon at the beach involves swimming or mostly walking on exposed sand.

## Facilities along the beach

Sun loungers, umbrellas and simple beachside warungs are dotted along most of the path, particularly near Sindhu and the central Segara stretch, so you rarely need to bring much beyond sun protection and a towel. Public toilets and showers are available at a few points but are not universal along the full five kilometres, so it is worth planning around the busier, more developed sections if that matters for your day.

## FAQ

### Where exactly is Sanur beach in Bali?
Sanur beach runs along Bali's east coast in Denpasar's Sanur district, roughly a five-kilometre stretch from the harbour area in the north down to Mertasari Beach in the south, all connected by a paved beachfront path.

### Can you swim at Sanur beach?
Yes, and it is one of the better swimming beaches on the island for calm conditions, thanks to the offshore reef. Always check tide and current conditions, as sandbanks and shallow reef sections can make some spots better than others depending on the tide.

### Is Sanur beach good for surfing?
Not really — the offshore reef that makes the water calm also blocks most of the swell. Surfers generally head to Canggu, Uluwatu or Kuta instead.

### Are there villas directly on Sanur beach?
Some villas sit a short walk from the beachfront path rather than directly on the sand, since most of the immediate beachfront is a public path and hotel frontage. Ask specifically about walking distance when comparing listings.

### What is the best time of day to visit Sanur beach?
Early morning is popular with locals for exercise and is cooler and quieter; late afternoon brings a pleasant light along the path, though it is not known for dramatic west-facing sunsets the way Canggu or Uluwatu are.
