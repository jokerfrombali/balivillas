Canggu's beach club scene, concentrated mostly around Berawa, ranges from relaxed daytime pool clubs to larger venues with DJs, daybeds and a full evening programme. The right choice depends on whether you want a quiet afternoon by a pool, a social scene with music, or something in between — and understanding what's typically free versus what costs extra saves a surprised bill at the end of the day.

## How Canggu beach clubs generally work

Most operate on a minimum-spend or entry-fee model rather than a flat cover charge — you pay either a small entry that's credited toward food and drink, or nothing to enter but a minimum spend at your table or daybed. Daybeds and prime pool-facing loungers usually cost extra or require a minimum spend commitment, while general lounger areas are often free with a drink order.

Policies vary by venue and by day (weekday versus weekend, and whether there's a scheduled DJ event), so check the specific club's current terms before you go rather than assuming a flat rule across all of them.

## Daytime versus evening programming

Daytime at most clubs is calmer — pool time, food, casual music — while several ramp up into a more club-like atmosphere from late afternoon into the evening, with DJs and a younger, louder crowd. If you want a relaxed family or couple's day by the pool, going earlier and leaving before the evening shift changes the whole experience.

## What to check before booking a table or daybed

- Confirm whether a booking fee or minimum spend applies, and whether it's credited toward your bill
- Ask if children are welcome, and during which hours, if you're travelling with kids
- Check whether the pool is open to non-members/non-diners or is exclusively for paying guests
- Look at the day's schedule — some days run DJ events that change the atmosphere significantly

## Beach clubs and families

Not every Canggu beach club is set up for children, and some explicitly lean adult-focused, particularly in the evening. If you're travelling with kids, check specifically whether the venue welcomes children during the hours you plan to visit, and whether pool depth and safety features suit younger swimmers — this varies significantly between venues.

## Getting there and getting back

Most beach clubs are a short scooter or car ride from villas across Canggu, and traffic near the busiest clubs can back up at peak arrival and departure times (late afternoon into evening). If you're planning to drink, arrange a driver rather than a scooter — this is worth arranging in advance during high season when demand for rides spikes near popular venues.

## Food and drink quality versus cost

Beach club food and drink prices generally run higher than a local warung or casual restaurant, reflecting the setting, service and often live entertainment rather than purely the ingredients. It's not unreasonable pricing for what it is, but it's worth going in with the right expectation rather than comparing it to a street-side meal.

Some clubs run happy hour or off-peak pricing on drinks during quieter afternoon windows, worth asking about if you're planning a longer pool day rather than a big evening out.

## Choosing between several beach clubs on the same trip

If you're staying long enough to visit more than one beach club, it's worth deliberately choosing different styles — a relaxed daytime pool day at one, a livelier evening at another — rather than repeating the same type of day. Berawa alone has enough variety in atmosphere between its different clubs that this is easy to do without leaving the neighbourhood.

## FAQ

### Are Canggu beach clubs expensive?

Costs vary by venue and vary further by whether you're paying a flat entry, a minimum spend, or just ordering from the menu with free entry. Check the specific club's current policy before your visit rather than assuming a fixed cost across all of them.

### Are Canggu beach clubs good for families with kids?

Some are, particularly during daytime hours, but not all — several lean adult-focused, especially in the evening. Check directly with the venue about their kids policy and pool safety features for your visit time.

### What's the difference between Canggu and Uluwatu beach clubs?

Uluwatu's clifftop clubs generally offer more dramatic ocean views given the terrain, while Canggu's are mostly beach-level around Berawa with a more social, pool-club atmosphere. Both scenes are popular; the choice often comes down to whether you want cliffside views or beach-level access.

### Do I need to book a table at a Canggu beach club in advance?

For weekends, holidays or scheduled DJ events, yes, it's worth booking ahead, since daybeds and prime tables fill up. For a quieter weekday visit, walking in is usually fine.

### How do I get to a beach club safely if I'm drinking?

Arrange a driver rather than riding a scooter after drinking. This is worth booking in advance during busy periods, since demand for rides near popular beach clubs spikes at peak departure times in the late afternoon and evening.
