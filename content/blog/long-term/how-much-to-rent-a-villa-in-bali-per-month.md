There's no single "Bali villa monthly rental cost" figure worth quoting, because a one-bedroom townhouse in Umalas and a four-bedroom pool villa in Sanur sit on completely different price scales. What you can rely on instead is a set of drivers — area, bedroom count, pool size, furnishing quality and season — that explain most of the spread you'll see between quotes.

We quote monthly rates on request for the 20 villas we manage across Sanur, Canggu, Umalas and Ungasan, and the pattern holds across almost every villa we've priced: ask two questions first — how many bedrooms, and which area — and you'll be within a sensible range before a single number is mentioned.

## What actually moves the price

- **Bedroom count.** This is the single biggest driver. Our range runs from 1-bedroom villas up to a 9-bedroom resort-style property, and monthly rates scale with it, though not perfectly linearly — larger villas often have a slightly better cost-per-bedroom than the smallest ones.
- **Area.** Sanur, Umalas, Canggu and Ungasan each carry a different market rate, shaped by land cost, proximity to the beach and local demand. Neither is "cheaper" across the board — it depends on the specific property.
- **Season.** High season (roughly July–August and the December holidays) pushes demand up; quieter months typically bring more room to negotiate, especially for stays of a month or more.
- **Furnishing and extras.** A villa with a dedicated workspace, fast Wi-Fi, a full kitchen and daily housekeeping included commands a different rate to a bare-bones long-stay unit.
- **Length of stay.** Monthly rates are typically lower per night than short daily bookings, and yearly commitments typically bring the rate down further still — we advertise 10% off for weekly stays and 15% off for monthly stays as a starting point, on top of whatever the base rate for that villa and season is.

## A realistic way to budget

Rather than anchor on a single number, think in terms of a range for your bedroom count and area, then adjust for season and extras:

| Villa size | What typically drives the range | How to narrow it down |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 bedroom | Area, whether the pool is private or shared, workspace quality | Ask for quotes across 2–3 specific villas in your target area |
| 3–4 bedroom | Area, garden size, number of en-suite bathrooms | Compare like-for-like bedroom count between areas |
| 5+ bedroom (group/resort-style) | Total capacity, extra amenities (cinema, multiple living areas) | Usually quoted per villa, not per bedroom, so ask directly |

Rents are genuinely indicative until you ask for a live quote against real dates — occupancy, season and any current promotions all move the number, and we'd rather give you an honest current figure over WhatsApp than a stale published rate.

## Monthly versus other durations

Monthly rentals sit between short daily stays and full-year leases in both price and flexibility. You typically pay more per night than a yearly lease works out to, but far less than daily rates, with the freedom to leave without breaking a long lease. If you're weighing a month against a full year, our comparison of [monthly or yearly villa rental](/blog/long-term/monthly-vs-yearly-villa-rental-bali/) walks through the trade-offs in more depth, and [yearly rental costs](/blog/long-term/bali-villa-yearly-rental-cost/) covers the other end of that comparison.

## What's usually included, and what isn't

Most monthly villa rates we quote include the property itself, standard utilities allowance (check the specific terms, as heavy air-conditioning use can push electricity above a base allowance), Wi-Fi, and either included or optional housekeeping depending on the villa. Extras to clarify before you commit: extra guest fees beyond the stated capacity, a security deposit, and whether staff costs (a dedicated cook or driver, for instance) are separate.

## What we'd tell someone budgeting for the first time

Get quotes for two or three specific villas that match your bedroom count in your target area before settling on a number in your head — published "starting from" rates elsewhere on the internet are often for the smallest, most basic unit in a portfolio and don't represent what a comparable family villa actually costs. Ask what's included in the quote, not just the headline figure, since utilities and staff terms vary enough between villas to change the real monthly cost meaningfully.

## How to compare quotes fairly between villas

When you have two or three monthly quotes in hand, compare them line by line rather than by the headline figure alone. A villa quoted slightly higher but including full utilities, weekly housekeeping and a dedicated workspace can work out better value than a cheaper quote that excludes all three. Ask each manager or owner for the same breakdown — base rent, utilities terms, housekeeping frequency, staff if any, and the security deposit — so you're comparing the same set of inclusions rather than guessing at what's bundled in.

## Why published "starting from" rates can mislead

Many villa portfolios advertise a "from" price pulled from their smallest, most basic unit, which sets an anchor that a family-sized villa in the same portfolio won't come close to matching. This is worth keeping in mind whenever you compare rates across different companies' listings, not just ours — the only reliable comparison is a live quote for a specific villa that matches your actual bedroom count, area and dates.

## FAQ

### How much does a villa cost per month in Bali?
It depends heavily on area and bedroom count, from smaller one-bedroom units up to large family or group villas, and varies further by season. Rather than rely on a published "from" price, ask for a live quote against your dates for two or three specific villas that match what you need.

### Is it cheaper to rent monthly than daily in Bali?
Yes, typically. Monthly rates are lower per night than short daily bookings, and we advertise a 15% discount off the base rate for monthly stays as a starting point, with the exact figure depending on the villa and season.

### What's usually included in a monthly villa rate in Bali?
Most quotes include the property, Wi-Fi, and either included or optional housekeeping, with a standard utilities allowance. Always confirm whether heavy air-conditioning use, extra guests, staff costs and the security deposit are included or separate before committing.

### Does the price change by area in Bali?
Yes — Sanur, Canggu, Umalas and Ungasan each carry different market rates driven by land cost and local demand, and no single area is consistently cheaper across every villa; the specific property matters as much as the area.

### Is it cheaper to rent a villa in low season?
Generally yes, and there's typically more room to negotiate for a month-long stay outside July–August and the December holidays, when demand is lower.

### How do I get an accurate monthly quote?
Contact us directly with your target area, bedroom count and dates — we quote rates on request the same day, which gives you a far more accurate number than any published "starting from" figure.
