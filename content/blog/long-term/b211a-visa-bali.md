The B211A visa Indonesia is a socio-cultural or single-entry business visit visa, sat between a standard tourist visa and a full residency permit like a KITAS. It is the visa most commonly used by people planning a Bali stay of a few months who are not working locally for an Indonesian company, do not yet qualify for a KITAS, but need longer than a standard tourist visa on arrival allows. It is not a work permit and does not let you take local employment (as of 2026, verify current validity period and extension terms on imigrasi.go.id, Indonesia's official immigration site, since visa categories and durations are updated periodically).

Most people apply through a licensed visa agent rather than directly, since the application requires a local sponsor and the paperwork is more involved than a straightforward tourist visa on arrival.

## What the B211A is generally used for

- A longer stay in Bali than a standard tourist visa allows, without yet committing to a KITAS
- Business visits — meetings, exploring investment, attending conferences — without taking local employment
- A bridge while sorting out a longer-term visa pathway, such as a KITAS application
- Property viewing trips for people considering a purchase, without yet needing residency status

It is explicitly not for local employment with an Indonesian company, which requires a different work-permit-linked visa and KITAS pathway entirely.

## How it compares to other common options

| Visa type | Typical duration approach | Who it suits |
|---|---|---|
| Standard tourist visa | Short stay, on arrival or e-visa | Holidaymakers, short visits |
| B211A | Longer visit visa, extendable, sponsor required | Longer stays not yet needing residency, business visits |
| Digital nomad visa (E33G) | Aimed at remote workers earning from abroad | Remote workers meeting income/eligibility criteria |
| KITAS | Full limited-stay residency permit | Those with a qualifying basis — investment, retirement, marriage, work |

If your stay is genuinely open-ended or you plan to be in Bali more than half the year on an ongoing basis, a KITAS pathway is usually the better fit long-term; the B211A is better suited to a defined, extendable but ultimately finite stay. ## Applying through a sponsor and agent

The B211A generally requires a local sponsor, which in practice is almost always arranged through a licensed visa agent rather than found independently. Choosing a reputable agent matters: ask for their current success rate, turnaround time, and exactly what the sponsor arrangement involves, since this is an area where a poorly chosen agent can cause real delays or complications.

- Confirm the agent is licensed and has a track record with B211A specifically, not just tourist visas
- Ask for the full current cost breakdown, including any extension fees
- Clarify what documentation you need to provide versus what the agent/sponsor handles
- Understand the extension process and any limits on how many times it can be extended

## Extensions and what happens when the visa runs out

B211A visas are generally extendable within Indonesia rather than requiring you to leave and re-enter, though the number of possible extensions and the total maximum stay are set by current immigration policy and worth confirming with your agent before you plan a stay length around it (as of 2026, verify with imigrasi.go.id or your visa agent). Plan your Bali stay with a buffer around the visa's actual expiry, not right up to the last possible day, since processing delays do happen.

## Common mistakes people make with the B211A

The most frequent mistake is treating it as a de facto work visa because the enforcement of "no local employment" is not always visible day to day — this is a real legal risk, not a grey area to exploit. The second common mistake is not planning the extension timeline properly and ending up scrambling near expiry, which can mean rushed decisions or overstaying risk.

## FAQ

### How long can I stay in Bali on a B211A visa?
The initial validity and total extendable period are set by current Indonesian immigration policy and can change, so confirm the current figures with your visa agent or on imigrasi.go.id before planning your stay length around it (as of 2026, verify).

### Can I work remotely for a foreign company on a B211A visa?
The B211A is not designed as a work visa and does not authorise local employment with an Indonesian company. Remote work for a foreign employer sits in a genuinely grey area under current rules, which is one reason the digital nomad E33G visa was introduced as a more defined alternative.

### Do I need a sponsor to apply for a B211A visa?
Yes, generally, and in practice this is almost always arranged through a licensed visa agent rather than found independently. Choosing a reputable, experienced agent is one of the most important decisions in the application process.

### Is the B211A better than a KITAS for a Bali stay?
It depends on your situation. The B211A suits a defined, extendable but ultimately finite stay without a qualifying basis for residency. A KITAS suits an open-ended stay where you have a qualifying basis such as investment, retirement, marriage or employment.

### Can I extend a B211A visa without leaving Indonesia?
Generally yes, extensions are typically processed within Indonesia, though the number of extensions and total maximum stay are set by current policy (as of 2026, verify with your agent). Plan extension timing with a buffer rather than right at the expiry date.
