The digital nomad visa Indonesia E33G was introduced specifically to give remote workers a defined legal category, rather than relying on the greyer territory of working remotely while on a B211A or tourist visa. It is aimed at people earning income from a foreign employer or their own business registered outside Indonesia, explicitly not for anyone taking local Indonesian income or working for an Indonesian company (as of 2026, verify current eligibility criteria, income requirements and validity period on imigrasi.go.id or with a licensed visa agent, since this is a newer category and details have been refined since introduction).

If your income comes entirely from clients, an employer or a business based outside Indonesia, this is generally the more defined and appropriate visa category to look at than continuing to stretch a B211A for remote work purposes.

## Who it is actually designed for

- Employees working remotely for a company based outside Indonesia
- Freelancers and contractors with foreign clients, paid into a foreign account
- Business owners running a company registered outside Indonesia, with no local Indonesian income
- Not designed for anyone earning income from Indonesian sources or clients

## E33G versus B211A for remote workers

| Factor | E33G digital nomad visa | B211A |
|---|---|---|
| Designed for remote work | Yes, explicitly | Not designed for this, legally ambiguous |
| Local income allowed | No | No |
| Typical applicant | Remote employee/freelancer with foreign income | Broader group: business visitors, property viewers, bridge to KITAS |
| Clarity of legal standing for remote work | Clearer, purpose-built | Greyer area if used specifically for remote work |

For anyone whose main reason for a Bali stay is remote work, the E33G is generally the cleaner legal fit, provided you meet its income and documentation requirements. Our guide to the B211A visa covers that broader category if your situation is different.

## Tax considerations: what to actually check

Whether and how your foreign income becomes taxable in Indonesia once you hold this visa and spend a significant part of the year in the country is a genuinely important question and depends on Indonesian tax residency rules, which are separate from immigration status (as of 2026, verify current tax residency thresholds and treatment with a qualified Indonesian tax adviser, not a visa agent, since this is a specialist area). Do not assume holding a digital nomad visa exempts you from Indonesian tax residency considerations; get proper advice before you commit to a long stay under this visa.

## What it does not give you

The E33G is a stay permit tied to remote work eligibility, not a path to permanent residency on its own, and it does not authorise local Indonesian employment or business activity generating local income. If your plans shift toward starting a local business or taking Indonesian-sourced income, you would need a different visa category, such as an investor KITAS, which our guide to investor KITAS Indonesia E28A covers.

## FAQ

### Who qualifies for the Indonesia digital nomad visa E33G?
Broadly, people earning income remotely from a foreign employer or their own foreign-registered business, with no local Indonesian income. Specific eligibility and income requirements are set by current immigration policy (as of 2026, verify with imigrasi.go.id or a licensed agent).

### Does the digital nomad visa mean I do not pay Indonesian tax?
Not necessarily. Tax residency in Indonesia is governed separately from immigration status and depends on factors like how much of the year you spend in the country. Get advice from a qualified Indonesian tax adviser rather than assuming the visa exempts you from tax residency rules.

### Is the E33G better than a B211A for remote workers?
Generally yes, since it is purpose-built for remote work with foreign income, giving clearer legal standing than using a B211A for the same purpose. If your situation differs, such as needing a business visa for other activities, a B211A may still fit better.

### Can I work for an Indonesian company on a digital nomad visa?
No. The E33G is designed for income from foreign sources only. Taking Indonesian-sourced income or working for a local company would require a different visa and work permit pathway entirely.

### How long is the digital nomad visa valid for?
