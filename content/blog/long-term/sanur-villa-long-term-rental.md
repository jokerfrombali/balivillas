Sanur is the area we'd point most families and quieter long-stayers toward first. It has 11 of our 20 villas, more than anywhere else we manage, which reflects the area's steady demand from exactly this kind of tenant — people staying months or a year, not a week.

The area's east-facing coastline sits behind a reef, which keeps the water calmer than Bali's west-coast surf beaches, and a paved beachfront path runs for several kilometres past cafés, small hotels and the harbour. For a long stay, that combination — calm water plus a walkable daily routine — matters more than it might for a short holiday.

## What villa styles are available long term

Our Sanur villas range from 2-bedroom pool townhouses up to 4-bedroom family villas with larger gardens, several within reach of the beach path on foot. Bedrooms in our current Sanur portfolio run from 2 to 4, with private and, in a couple of cases, shared pools between neighbouring villas. Properties like Villa Black Pool (4br) and the Sanur Garden Pool Villa (4br, near Mertasari Beach) suit families wanting real space; smaller 2-bedroom townhouses like the Stylish or Modern Pool Townhouses suit couples or small groups on a long stay.

## Daily life in Sanur for a long-stay tenant

Sanur has a genuinely different rhythm from Canggu or Kuta — fewer beach clubs and nightlife, more long lunches, quiet evenings and a community of long-term residents and repeat visitors who come back year after year. It's easier to settle into a routine here: a regular café, a walk or cycle along the beach path, and a predictable, manageable level of traffic compared with the busier tourist strips further north and west.

## What a typical week looks like for a long-stay tenant

Long-term tenants in Sanur tend to settle into a routine built around the beach path — an early walk or cycle before the heat sets in, breakfast at a regular café, a working morning if remote work is part of the stay, and an evening walk back along the water as the light softens. Weekends often mean a short trip out (Nusa Penida by fast boat from the harbour, or inland to Ubud), but the daily rhythm stays local, which is part of why families and longer-term tenants report settling in faster here than in busier areas.

## FAQ

### Is Sanur good for long-term villa rental?
Yes — it's the area we manage the most villas in, largely because of steady demand from families and long-stay tenants who want calmer water, a walkable beach path and a quieter daily pace than Canggu or Kuta.

### How much does a long-term villa rental cost in Sanur?
It depends on bedroom count and season; our Sanur villas range from 2-bedroom townhouses to 4-bedroom family villas, and we quote current monthly and yearly rates on request rather than publishing figures that go stale.

### Do I need a scooter to live in Sanur long term?
Not necessarily for daily errands — the beachfront path makes walking or cycling realistic for many trips. You'll likely still want a taxi app or occasional driver for trips further afield.

### Is Sanur good for families on a long-term stay?
Yes, it's the area we'd recommend first for families among the four we manage villas in, due to the calmer, reef-protected water and quieter residential lanes compared with the busier surf and nightlife areas.

### How does Sanur compare to Canggu for long-term rental?
Sanur is quieter, calmer and more walkable; Canggu has more nightlife, surf and a younger working-holiday crowd. Neither is objectively better — it depends on the pace you want for your stay.

### Can I rent monthly in Sanur and switch to yearly later?
Yes, this is a common path for tenants who want to confirm the area and villa suit them before committing to a full year — ask us about starting monthly with the option to convert.
