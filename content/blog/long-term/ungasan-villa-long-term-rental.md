Ungasan sits on the elevated Bukit peninsula in Bali's far south, near Melasti and Green Bowl beaches, and it's the quietest of the four areas we manage villas in. Long-term tenants come here for the combination of clifftop views, calm and proximity to some of Bali's most dramatic coastline — not for walkable convenience, which the area doesn't really offer.

We manage 4 villas in Ungasan, all built from natural teak with private pools, reflecting a distinct style compared with our Sanur, Canggu and Umalas properties.

## What villa styles are available in Ungasan

Our Ungasan villas are compact, teak-built 1- and 2-bedroom homes: Eco Private, Eco Exclusive and Eco Tropical are 1-bedroom villas with private pools, free-standing tubs and garden views, while Eco Teak is a 2-bedroom option with two en-suite bathrooms. All four are built from natural teak, giving them a distinct character from the more contemporary pool townhouses we manage in Umalas or the larger family villas in Sanur. This makes Ungasan a strong fit for couples or small households on a long stay, less so for larger families needing several bedrooms.

## Daily life in Ungasan for a long-stay tenant

Life in Ungasan moves at a different pace from the rest of south Bali's tourist areas — longer, quieter lanes, dramatic limestone cliffs, and beach access at spots like Melasti and Green Bowl that require a short but real drive rather than a walk. Long-term tenants here tend to value the quiet and the view over daily convenience, and almost everyone staying in our Ungasan villas uses a scooter, car or driver to get around.

## What long-term tenants ask us most

- **Do I need a scooter to live in Ungasan?** Yes, essentially — the clifftop lanes are longer and more spread out than Sanur's or Umalas's, and reaching beaches and restaurants comfortably needs transport.
- **Is Ungasan good for families?** It suits couples and small households better than larger families, given the 1- and 2-bedroom villa stock we manage there.
- **How far is Ungasan from the airport?** Typically 30–40 minutes depending on traffic, similar to most of south Bali.
- **Should I compare it to a closer-in area first?** Worth it if daily convenience matters as much as quiet — see our [how to find a long-term rental](/blog/long-term/how-to-find-long-term-villa-rental-bali/) guide for a broader area-by-area approach.

## What a typical week looks like for a long-stay tenant

Life in Ungasan moves around the clifftop rather than a café strip — a drive down to Melasti or Green Bowl for a swim, meals either cooked at the villa or at one of the restaurants that have opened along the main roads in recent years, and long, quiet evenings with the sound of the sea rather than traffic. It suits tenants who've deliberately chosen distance from the busier strips, and who don't mind planning trips out a little further in advance than they would in Sanur or Umalas.

## FAQ

### Is Ungasan good for long-term rental?
Yes, particularly for couples or small households who want quiet, clifftop views and proximity to Melasti and Green Bowl beaches. It's the quietest of the four areas we manage villas in.

### Do I need a scooter or car to live in Ungasan?
Essentially yes — the area's lanes are longer and more spread out than Sanur's or Umalas's, and reaching beaches and restaurants comfortably needs transport.

### What villa sizes does The Host Bali manage in Ungasan?
We manage four teak-built villas in Ungasan, three 1-bedroom and one 2-bedroom, each with a private pool — suited to couples or small households rather than large families.

### Is Ungasan far from the airport?
Typically 30–40 minutes by car depending on traffic, broadly similar to Sanur and Canggu, since all three sit within south Bali's tourist belt.

### Is Ungasan good for families on a long-term stay?
It can suit smaller families, but the villa stock we manage there skews toward 1- and 2-bedroom homes, so larger families often do better in Sanur or with one of our bigger Canggu villas.

### How does Ungasan compare to Uluwatu for long-term rental?
Ungasan and Uluwatu sit on the same Bukit peninsula and share a similar quiet, clifftop character; Ungasan specifically is closer to Melasti and Green Bowl beaches, which is where our own villas are concentrated.
