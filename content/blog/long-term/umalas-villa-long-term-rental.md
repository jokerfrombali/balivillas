Umalas is the quieter neighbour to Canggu, sitting inland between it and Seminyak, and it's become a go-to for long-stay tenants who want the same café and coworking access without Canggu's beach-road congestion. It's a smaller market than Sanur for us — we manage 3 villas there — but demand from monthly and yearly tenants is consistently strong.

The area is built around quiet residential lanes with a growing cluster of cafés, small shops and coworking spaces within easy reach, giving it a different daily rhythm from either Canggu's busier strip or Sanur's beachfront pace.

## What villa styles are available in Umalas

Our Umalas villas range from compact townhouse-style pool homes to larger family properties: Premier Umalas 16 is a 3-bedroom pool townhouse with all-en-suite bedrooms and a desk in every room, Premier Umalas 17 is a 2-bedroom pool home with two dedicated workspaces, and Villa Papat is a larger 4-bedroom pool villa with a tropical garden and open living room facing the pool, suited to bigger groups or families. The two Premier villas are set up with fast Wi-Fi (200 Mbps) and air conditioning throughout, reflecting Umalas's role as a base for people working from their villa as much as relaxing in it.

## Daily life in Umalas for a long-stay tenant

Umalas suits a routine built around working from home with occasional trips to a café or coworking space nearby, rather than daily beach-club visits. It's quieter than Canggu day to day, with less nightlife but easy access to it a short ride away. Long-term tenants here tend to value the combination of workspace quality inside the villa and a calm lane to actually live on.

## What a typical week looks like for a long-stay tenant

A long-term tenant in Umalas often builds their week around the villa itself more than in Canggu — a morning working from the desk in the bedroom or a nearby café, a short ride into Canggu or Seminyak for a change of scenery a few times a week, and quieter evenings back in the lane. It suits people who want to be near the action without living inside it, which is the area's main appeal compared with its busier neighbour.

## FAQ

### Is Umalas good for long-term rental?
Yes, particularly for remote workers who want café and coworking access without Canggu's traffic. It's quieter and more residential than Canggu while still close to it.

### What villa sizes does The Host Bali manage in Umalas?
We manage three villas in Umalas: two pool townhouses (3-bedroom and 2-bedroom) set up with dedicated workspaces and fast Wi-Fi, and a larger 4-bedroom pool villa, Villa Papat, suited to bigger groups or families.

### Is Umalas close to Canggu?
Yes, Umalas sits directly between Seminyak and Canggu, giving easy access to both without the traffic of living directly on Canggu's beach road.

### Is Umalas walkable for daily errands?
Partially — many properties are within a 10–15 minute walk of a growing café and small-shop cluster, though it's less continuously walkable than Sanur's beachfront path.

### Is Umalas good for families on a long-term stay?
It can work, but it's not beachfront and suits remote workers and couples slightly better than young families, who often do better in Sanur's calmer, more residential setting.

### How does Umalas compare to Canggu for cost?
Umalas generally sits a notch below Canggu's premium beachside pricing, reflecting its inland position, while offering similar access to cafés and coworking space nearby.
