A domicile letter, known locally as surat domisili or surat keterangan domisili, is a document confirming where you are living in Bali, typically issued at the village (desa or kelurahan) level. Long-stay renters and residents often need one for administrative steps such as opening a local bank account, registering a vehicle, or supporting certain visa or tax processes (as of 2026, confirm the exact current process and which office issues it with a licensed visa agent, since local administrative procedures vary and are updated periodically).

This guide covers what a domicile letter is used for, who typically needs one, and how the process generally works for a long-stay villa renter.

## What a domicile letter is actually for

Think of it as proof of local address, similar in function to a utility bill or lease agreement used for identity verification in other countries, but formalised through the local village administration. It is commonly requested when opening certain bank accounts, applying for an NPWP (Indonesian tax number), registering a vehicle, or as supporting documentation for some visa processes. Our guide on [NPWP for expats in Bali](/blog/long-term/npwp-for-expats-bali/) covers one of the more common reasons long-stay residents end up needing one.

## Who typically needs one

Most short-stay tourists never need a domicile letter. It becomes relevant once you are genuinely settling in, typically renters on a yearly lease, residents setting up local banking, or families going through the fuller relocation process. Our guide on [relocating to Bali with a family timeline](/blog/long-term/family-relocation-timeline-bali/) places the domicile letter step within the broader arrival sequence if you are still early in planning a move.

## What the process generally involves

The general pattern is that your landlord or the village office where your rental is located supports the application, since it confirms an actual address, so a cooperative landlord matters here. A registered visa agent can also handle this on your behalf alongside other paperwork, which is often the more practical route for renters unfamiliar with the local administrative system. Confirm the current specific steps, required documents and any fee with a licensed agent or directly with the relevant village office, since the exact process can vary by area and is reviewed periodically.

## What a domicile letter is commonly used to support

| Use case | Why it comes up |
|---|---|
| Opening a local bank account | Many banks ask for local proof of address |
| Applying for an NPWP tax number | Supports registration as a taxpayer |
| Vehicle registration | Confirms an address for ownership records |
| Some visa or KITAS-related steps | Used as supporting documentation in certain processes |

## What to do before you need one urgently

- Ask your landlord early in a yearly lease whether they can support a domicile letter application
- Confirm with a licensed visa agent which specific document you actually need for your situation, since requirements are sometimes conflated
- Keep your lease agreement and passport copies organised, since most administrative steps ask for the same core documents repeatedly
- Do not assume a domicile letter substitutes for your visa or KITAS status; it is a separate, additional document

## If your rental situation changes

If you later end your rental or move to a different area, your domicile letter and any linked registrations, such as a bank account tied to that address, may need updating. ## FAQ

### Do I need a domicile letter as a short-term tourist in Bali?
No, a domicile letter is generally only relevant for longer-stay residents dealing with local administrative steps like banking, tax registration or vehicle ownership, not for short holiday stays.

### Who issues a domicile letter in Bali?
It is typically issued at the village (desa or kelurahan) level where you are renting, though the exact current process should be confirmed with a licensed visa agent or the local office, since procedures vary and are reviewed periodically (as of 2026, verify).

### Does my landlord need to be involved in getting a domicile letter?
Often yes, since the letter confirms your actual address and a cooperative landlord makes the process considerably easier. Ask about this before signing a yearly lease if you expect to need one.

### Is a domicile letter the same as a KITAS or visa?
No, it is a separate local address registration document, not a substitute for your visa or KITAS status. Some administrative processes ask for both, so do not assume one replaces the other.

### What do I need a domicile letter for most commonly?
Opening a local bank account and applying for an NPWP tax number are the two most common reasons long-stay renters in Bali end up needing one, alongside some vehicle registration and visa-related processes.
