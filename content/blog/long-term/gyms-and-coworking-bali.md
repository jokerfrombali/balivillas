Gyms and coworking in Bali cover a wide range of price and style, and the right choice depends more on your actual weekly routine than on which option looks best online. Canggu and Umalas have the densest concentration of both, often combined into single membership clubs offering a gym, pool, workspace and café under one roof. Sanur and Ungasan have fewer options but enough for a workable routine if you are not chasing the most premium facilities.

Before committing to a membership, visit during the hours you would actually use it, since a gym or coworking space that looks great in photos can be crowded, noisy or short on desks exactly when you need it most.

## Matching the option to your routine, not the marketing

A premium wellness club with a full gym, classes, a pool and a coworking floor suits someone who wants one membership covering most of their week. A simple, no-frills gym paired with a separate, cheaper coworking desk suits someone who wants to control cost more tightly or who already has a good villa workspace and only needs the gym separately.

| Type | Typical strengths | Typical trade-offs |
|---|---|---|
| All-in-one wellness club | Gym, workspace, pool, community events in one place | Higher monthly cost |
| Standalone gym | Lower cost, straightforward | No workspace, may lack variety of equipment |
| Dedicated coworking space | Reliable desks, meeting rooms, strong internet backup | Separate cost from gym, may need a second membership |
| Villa-based routine | No extra membership cost, full flexibility | Needs self-discipline, less community and equipment variety |

## What to check before signing a membership

- Visit at your actual planned usage time, not an off-peak demo slot
- Ask about contract length and whether monthly, rather than only annual, membership is available
- For coworking, ask specifically about desk availability during peak hours and internet backup quality
- For gyms, check equipment variety and whether classes are included or charged separately
- Ask about guest passes if you expect visitors during a long stay

## Fitness routines beyond the gym

Bali's outdoor options — surfing in Canggu, running along Sanur's beach path, yoga studios across most areas — mean a gym membership is not the only route to staying active during a long stay. Many long-stayers build a routine mixing a gym or coworking membership with outdoor activity specific to their area, which is worth factoring into the decision of where to base yourself in the first place.

## Cost and value across the areas

Costs vary by area and by the specific club or space, generally trending higher in Canggu and Umalas given the concentration of premium options there, with more budget-friendly choices available in Sanur and further from the main tourist corridors. ## FAQ

### Which area of Bali has the best gyms and coworking spaces?
Canggu and Umalas have the densest concentration of both, often combined into premium wellness clubs. Sanur and Ungasan have fewer options but enough for a workable routine, generally at a more moderate cost.

### Do I need a separate gym and coworking membership, or is there a combined option?
Both exist. All-in-one wellness clubs, common in Canggu and Umalas, combine gym, workspace and often a pool under one membership. Standalone gyms and dedicated coworking spaces are usually cheaper individually but require two separate memberships.

### Are monthly memberships available, or only long-term contracts?
Most gyms and coworking spaces in Bali's main areas offer monthly options alongside longer contracts, though pricing usually improves with a longer commitment. Ask directly about monthly rates if you are not certain how long your stay will run.

### Can I work from a coworking space instead of my villa full time?
Yes, and some long-stayers prefer this for the internet reliability, meeting rooms and social structure it offers. Others use it only occasionally alongside villa-based work. Either approach works; it depends on your need for structure versus flexibility.

### Do gyms in Bali typically include classes, or are they charged separately?
It varies by gym and membership tier. Some include a full class schedule, others charge per class or reserve classes for a higher membership tier. Confirm this directly before comparing prices between two gyms, since headline membership cost alone is not a fair comparison.
