The silver hair visa is Indonesia's newer, distinct long-stay category aimed at older foreign residents, and it is easy to confuse with the standard retirement visa because both target retirees. The two have separate eligibility criteria and terms, so if you are planning a long-stay villa rental around one, it is worth confirming which one actually fits your circumstances before you commit to a lease.

We are not visa specialists, we manage villas, but because so many of our monthly and yearly guests arrive on one of these permits, this guide covers the practical side: what the silver hair visa is, how it differs from the alternatives, and what it means for your rental.

## What sets the silver hair visa apart

Where the standard retirement visa has long-established, generally lower financial thresholds, the silver hair visa is typically pitched at a different eligibility band and duration, sometimes offering longer validity in exchange for higher financial proof (as of 2026, verify exact figures and terms with a licensed visa agent, since this is a newer category and details are periodically clarified). Both restrict local employment. The practical upshot for a renter is the same either way: you will generally need your visa status settled or clearly in progress before a landlord commits to a yearly contract.

## Why the visa route matters for your rental

A yearly or monthly villa contract in Bali is generally tied to your documented resident status, so the visa category you choose affects the paperwork a landlord asks for and, indirectly, how comfortable they are locking in a long lease with you. If your silver hair visa application is still being processed, be upfront about the timeline; some landlords, us included, are willing to work with an application clearly in progress, while others prefer to see the visa finalised first.

## FAQ

### Is the silver hair visa the same as the retirement visa?
No. They are separate categories with different eligibility criteria and terms, though both target older foreign residents who do not plan to work locally. Confirm which one you qualify for with a licensed visa agent before assuming either applies.

### What financial proof does the silver hair visa require?
Requirements are periodically reviewed and should be confirmed directly with a licensed agent rather than assumed from older sources; this guide deliberately does not quote a figure it cannot verify as current (as of 2026, verify).

### Can silver hair visa holders work remotely for a foreign employer?
Local employment is generally not permitted under this category, and remote work rules can be nuanced; if paid remote work is part of your plan, ask a visa agent whether a different category, such as a digital nomad visa, fits better.

### Do I need my silver hair visa finalised before signing a yearly villa lease?
It depends on the landlord. Some, including us, can work with an application clearly in progress, while others prefer the visa issued first. Be upfront about your timeline when discussing a lease.

### Which area in Bali suits silver hair visa holders best?
Sanur is generally the easiest base: flat walking paths, a calmer pace and an established set of shops and services nearby, similar to why it suits retirees and older couples generally.
