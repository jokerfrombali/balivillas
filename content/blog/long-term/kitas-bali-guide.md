KITAS Indonesia (Kartu Izin Tinggal Terbatas) is a limited-stay residency card, sitting above visit visas like the B211A in terms of what it lets you do — most notably opening the door to things like registering with a bank more easily and applying for an Indonesian driving licence conversion, neither of which a visit visa alone typically supports. It is not a single visa but a category covering several different pathways, each tied to a specific qualifying basis: investment, retirement, marriage to an Indonesian citizen, employment with an Indonesian-registered company, and a few other categories (as of 2026, verify current categories and requirements on imigrasi.go.id or with a licensed agent, since KITAS categories and requirements are periodically updated).

Which pathway applies to you depends entirely on your actual situation — there is no single "generic KITAS" application, so the first real decision is identifying which category genuinely fits before you engage an agent.

## The main KITAS pathways at a glance

| Pathway | Typical qualifying basis | Who it suits |
|---|---|---|
| Investor KITAS (E28A) | Investment in an Indonesian company (PT PMA) | Business owners, investors setting up locally |
| Retirement KITAS (E33F) | Meeting age and financial requirements | Retirees planning to live in Bali long-term |
| Marriage KITAS | Married to an Indonesian citizen | Spouses of Indonesian nationals |
| Work KITAS | Employment with an Indonesian-registered company holding a work permit | Employees of local companies |
| Silver Hair Visa (E33E) | Older applicants meeting specific financial criteria | Older long-stayers not pursuing full retirement KITAS |

## Why KITAS matters beyond just "staying longer"

Holding a KITAS generally opens up practical parts of daily life that a visit visa does not: it is usually a prerequisite for opening a local bank account in your own name, converting your driving licence, and it demonstrates a more settled residency status if you are dealing with schools, healthcare providers or landlords who want assurance about your length of stay. Our guides to opening a bank account in Bali as a foreigner and the Indonesian driving licence process both assume KITAS-level residency as the typical starting point.

## KITAS versus a visit visa like the B211A

The core difference is intent and duration: a B211A suits a defined, extendable but ultimately finite stay without a specific qualifying basis for residency, while a KITAS suits a genuinely open-ended situation where you have a specific qualifying basis — investment, retirement, marriage or employment. If you are still working out which basis applies to you, or do not yet have one, a B211A is often the practical starting point while you sort out longer-term plans.

## What the application process generally involves

- Identifying the correct pathway and qualifying basis for your situation
- Gathering the specific documentation for that pathway (investment proof, marriage certificate, employment contract, retirement financial proof, etc.)
- Engaging a licensed agent experienced with your specific KITAS category
- A sponsor requirement, which varies by pathway (a company for investor/work KITAS, a spouse for marriage KITAS)
- Renewal timing and requirements, which differ by category and are worth understanding before your first KITAS even issues

## Renewal and long-term planning

KITAS permits are typically limited-stay and require periodic renewal, with the exact validity period and renewal process varying by category and subject to change (as of 2026, verify current terms for your specific pathway). Some long-term residents eventually pursue KITAP (permanent stay permit) after holding KITAS status for a required period, which is a separate and longer-term consideration worth discussing with your agent once you are established.

## Choosing the right agent for your pathway

Because KITAS categories differ so much in their requirements, an agent experienced specifically in your pathway matters more than a generalist. Someone who mainly handles retirement KITAS applications may not be the best fit for an investor KITAS tied to a new PT PMA company, and vice versa. Ask directly about their specific track record in your category.

## FAQ

### What is the difference between a KITAS and a visa on arrival?
A visa on arrival or tourist visa is a short-stay entry document, while a KITAS is a limited-stay residency permit tied to a specific qualifying basis such as investment, retirement, marriage or employment, and it generally unlocks practical residency benefits like easier local bank account access.

### How many types of KITAS are there?
Several, each tied to a different qualifying basis: investor, retirement, marriage, employment and a few other specific categories including the silver hair visa for older applicants. The correct pathway depends entirely on your individual situation (as of 2026, verify current categories with a licensed agent).

### Does holding a KITAS let me work in Indonesia?
Only if your KITAS is tied to a valid work permit through an Indonesian-registered employer, or you hold an investor KITAS tied to your own PT PMA company. Other KITAS categories, such as retirement or marriage, do not automatically authorise local employment.

### How long does a KITAS application take?
Timelines vary by category and current processing capacity at immigration offices, and are worth confirming with your agent at the start of the process rather than assumed. Build in a buffer before any deadline, such as an existing visa's expiry, that depends on the KITAS being approved in time.

### Can a KITAS lead to permanent residency in Indonesia?
Some long-term KITAS holders become eligible for KITAP (permanent stay permit) after holding KITAS status for a required period, though the specific eligibility and process depend on your original KITAS category (as of 2026, verify current requirements with your agent).
