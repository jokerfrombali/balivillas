Living in Ungasan on a long-stay villa rental means quiet clifftop lanes, some of the closest access to Melasti and Green Bowl beaches on the Bukit, and a slower daily rhythm than Canggu or Umalas. Most errands, groceries, a wider choice of restaurants, medical care, involve a drive rather than a walk, so it suits residents who value privacy and scenery over daily convenience.

We manage four villas in Ungasan, so this is a first-hand account of what the area is actually like to live in, not just visit for a few nights.

## The daily rhythm

Ungasan is residential and spread out compared to Canggu or Umalas. Streets are quieter, traffic is lighter, and the pace is generally slower. Mornings tend to centre on the villa itself, the pool, a home-cooked breakfast, before heading out to a beach or a longer errand run. There is a smaller but growing set of cafés and warungs locally, though the wider choice of restaurants and shopping sits in Jimbaran or further towards Uluwatu, both a drive rather than a walk from most Ungasan villas.

## What it costs to live here

Long-term rental rates in Ungasan sit broadly in line with our other areas for comparable villa size and finish, though our smaller teak eco-villas here, one and two-bedroom homes built around privacy rather than space, offer a lower entry point than a similarly located villa in Canggu. ## Who Ungasan suits and who it does not

| Fits well | Less suited |
|---|---|
| Residents prioritising privacy and views | Those who want to walk to daily errands |
| Couples or small families on a slower routine | Large groups wanting a social, walkable base |
| People who drive or use a driver regularly | Anyone without reliable transport |
| Long-stay residents drawn to the Bukit's beaches | Remote workers needing dense coworking options nearby |

## Getting around from Ungasan

A scooter or car is close to essential here; walking covers the immediate villa area but not much beyond it. Most residents either keep a scooter for local trips or arrange a driver for beach days and errands further out. Our guide on [buying a scooter or car in Bali](/blog/long-term/buying-a-scooter-or-car-bali/) covers the practical side of setting this up once you have moved in.

## How Ungasan compares to Canggu and Umalas as a long-stay base

Canggu offers the opposite trade-off: dense, walkable, social, but noisier and more trafficked. Umalas sits in between, quieter than Canggu with better walkability than Ungasan. Our guides on [living in Canggu long-term](/blog/long-term/long-stay-in-canggu-what-its-like/) and [living in Umalas long-term](/blog/long-term/long-stay-in-umalas-what-its-like/) cover those two areas the same way this page covers Ungasan, so read whichever matches the priority that matters most to you, walkability versus privacy.

## What to check before committing to Ungasan long-term

- Confirm your own or a driver's transport plan before signing, since walking will not cover most errands
- Ask about internet reliability at the specific villa, as coverage can vary more here than in denser areas
- Check distance to the healthcare provider or pharmacy you would actually use regularly
- Visit at a different time of day than your viewing to get a real sense of the drive times

## FAQ

### Is Ungasan a good area to live in Bali long-term?
It suits residents who prioritise quiet, privacy and proximity to the Bukit's beaches over daily walkability. If you want to walk to restaurants and shops regularly, Umalas or Canggu are a better long-term fit.

### Do I need a car to live in Ungasan?
Effectively yes, either your own scooter or car, or a regular driver arrangement. Walking covers only the immediate area around most villas; groceries, a wider choice of dining and medical care generally require a drive.

### How does the cost of living in Ungasan compare to Canggu?
Broadly similar for comparable villa size, though our smaller Ungasan eco-villas offer a lower entry point than an equivalent Canggu villa. See our [yearly rental prices by area](/blog/long-term/yearly-villa-rental-prices-bali-by-area/) guide for indicative ranges.

### Is Ungasan good for remote workers?
It can work, but Umalas offers denser coworking options and a shorter drive to Canggu's café scene, which usually suits remote workers better unless quiet and privacy outweigh that convenience for you.

### What is the closest beach to most Ungasan villas?
Melasti and Green Bowl beaches are among the closest and most well known on the Bukit, both reachable by a short drive from most of our Ungasan villas.
