Ending a villa management agreement in Bali cleanly means working through your contract's notice period, resolving existing bookings, settling deposits and expenses, and transferring account and calendar access, in that order, with everything confirmed in writing before the handover date. Skipping steps is where owners end up with double-booked calendars or missing funds after a switch.

We take over management from previous managers regularly, and the exits that go smoothly are the ones where the owner worked through a proper checklist rather than just giving notice and hoping it sorted itself out.

## Start with the notice period, not the end date

Your contract will specify a notice period — read it before deciding on a target exit date, because ending earlier than the contract allows can create its own dispute, even if you're unhappy with the manager's performance. If the contract's notice period genuinely isn't being honoured, that becomes a dispute question — see our guide on [disputes with a villa management company](/blog/owners/villa-legal-disputes-with-manager/) — but the default should be working within the agreed notice period rather than around it.

## What happens to existing bookings

This is the part owners most often overlook. Any bookings already confirmed and paid for during the notice period, or extending past your planned exit date, need an explicit plan: does the outgoing manager fulfil them, does the new manager take over mid-stream, and how is payment for those bookings handled between the two parties? Get this agreed in writing before the transition date, not worked out as bookings arrive.

| Step | What to confirm in writing |
|---|---|
| Notice given | Date, method, contract clause referenced |
| Existing bookings | Who fulfils them, how payment is handled |
| Deposits held by manager | Amount, return timeline |
| Outstanding expenses | Final statement, any disputed items resolved |
| Listing accounts (Airbnb, Booking.com) | Ownership transfer or new listings |
| Keys, access codes, staff contacts | Handover date and method |

## Deposits and outstanding expenses

Ask for a final statement covering the period up to the exit date, itemised, before the handover is considered complete. If a security deposit or float was held by the manager for operational expenses, confirm the return amount and timeline in writing. Any disputed expense should be resolved, or at minimum formally documented as unresolved, before final handover — not left as a loose end that becomes harder to chase once the relationship has ended.

## Listing accounts and online presence

If the villa is listed on Airbnb, Booking.com or similar platforms under the outgoing manager's account, work out whether those listings transfer to the owner or new manager, or whether new listings need to be built from scratch — the latter means losing review history and search ranking built up over time, which is a real cost worth factoring into the exit timeline. This should be discussed and agreed before giving notice, ideally, since it can affect how much notice you actually want to give.

## Physical handover

- Confirm a specific date and time for the handover of keys and access codes
- Get a full inventory check against the original inventory, noting any discrepancies
- Collect contact details for any staff the new manager will retain
- Confirm any maintenance schedules or ongoing contracts (pool service, pest control) and whether they transfer or need renewing
- Do a final walkthrough together if possible, documenting the villa's condition at handover

## Onboarding a new manager in parallel

If you're switching to a new manager rather than self-managing, it helps to run onboarding in parallel with the exit process rather than sequentially — the new manager reviewing the villa, meeting retained staff and understanding the property before the old manager's access ends, rather than a gap where nobody is actively managing bookings and guest enquiries. This overlap period is worth planning explicitly into the notice-period timeline.

## When the relationship ended over a dispute

If the exit follows an unresolved dispute rather than a straightforward decision to move on, the handover conversation can be more difficult, and it's worth having any final settlement (deposits, disputed expenses) confirmed in writing even if that takes longer than a clean, amicable handover would. Rushing a handover to avoid an uncomfortable conversation often costs more later if something wasn't properly settled.

## What to do next

A clean exit from a villa management agreement is mostly a sequencing and documentation exercise: notice, existing bookings, deposits and expenses, listing accounts, then physical handover, each step confirmed in writing before moving to the next.

- Read your contract's notice and exit clauses before setting a target date
- Get a written plan for existing bookings during the transition period
- Request a final itemised statement and confirm deposit return before handover is complete

## FAQ

### How much notice do I need to give to end a villa management contract in Bali?
This depends entirely on your specific contract's notice clause, which is the first thing to check before planning an exit date. Ending outside the agreed notice period, even for good reason, can itself become a point of dispute.

### What happens to bookings already confirmed when I switch managers?
This needs an explicit written agreement between the outgoing and incoming manager (or owner) covering who fulfils each booking and how payment is handled. It should be settled before the transition date, not worked out as each booking arrives.

### Will I lose my villa's Airbnb or Booking.com reviews when I switch managers?
Possibly, if the listing was created under the outgoing manager's account and doesn't transfer. This is worth clarifying and agreeing before giving notice, since it affects how much review history and search ranking you might be starting over with.

### What should a final statement from an outgoing manager include?
An itemised breakdown of income and expenses up to the exit date, the deposit or float amount being returned and its timeline, and resolution or documentation of any disputed items. Don't consider the handover complete without this.

### Can I run onboarding with a new manager while still under contract with the old one?
Often yes, and it's a good idea where your contract allows it — having the new manager review the property and meet retained staff before the old manager's access ends avoids a gap in active management during the transition.
