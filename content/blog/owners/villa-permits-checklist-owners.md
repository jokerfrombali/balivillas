The permits needed to rent out a villa in Bali fall into four groups: business registration, the correct activity classification, tax registration, and a local tourism licence. Owners rarely need to chase all four from zero — a villa bought with rental history may already have some in place — but very few villas have all four confirmed and current without someone actually checking each one individually.

This is a working checklist rather than a legal filing guide. Treat it as the list to hand to a lawyer, notary or licensing agent so nothing gets assumed rather than confirmed.

## The four permit groups, in the order they usually get done

1. **NIB (Nomor Induk Berusaha)** — the core business registration number, obtained through the OSS (Online Single Submission) system
2. **KBLI code** — the specific business activity classification attached to the NIB that identifies the villa as tourist accommodation, not just any registered business
3. **NPWP** — the tax registration number the rental income gets reported against
4. **Local tourism licence** (commonly pondok wisata for smaller properties, with other categories for larger complexes) — issued at the regency or provincial level

Skipping ahead — for example applying for a tourism licence without a matching KBLI code already in place — tends to cause delays rather than save time, since the local licence usually asks for the business registration as a prerequisite document.

## What each one actually confirms

| Permit | What it confirms |
|---|---|
| NIB | The villa's rental activity is registered as a formal business |
| KBLI code | The specific business type matches tourist accommodation, not a generic classification |
| NPWP | The owner or entity is registered to report and pay tax on rental income |
| Local tourism licence | Local government has approved the specific property for tourist accommodation use |

## Documents to gather before starting

- Proof of land/property rights (leasehold agreement or equivalent)
- Building documentation matching what was actually constructed on site
- Passport or KITAS details for the registering individual, or company documents for a PT PMA
- Confirmation of the land's zoning or use classification

Missing or mismatched building documentation is one of the more common reasons a local tourism licence application stalls, particularly for villas that had unpermitted alterations made after original construction.

## Who typically applies for what

An owner can, in principle, work through all four registrations personally or through a notary. In practice, many owners coordinate this through whoever manages the villa day to day, since the local tourism licence step usually needs someone physically present to liaise with the regency office and, in some cases, the village or banjar. Foreign owners managing this remotely from abroad add a layer of delay that a locally based manager or agent can usually avoid.

## A realistic timeline expectation

Processing time varies by regency, by how complete the supporting documents are, and by current administrative backlog, and it is not unusual for the full sequence to take longer than an owner initially expects (as of 2026, verify current processing timelines with a licensed adviser, since this has fluctuated). Budgeting for weeks rather than days, and having documents ready before starting rather than gathering them mid-process, meaningfully shortens the real timeline.

## What happens if a villa is already renting without full permits

This is common enough that it is worth addressing directly rather than treating it as a special case. A villa already generating bookings without complete permits should not stop renting abruptly in most situations, but should treat registration as urgent and get a written timeline from whoever is coordinating it. ## FAQ

### What permits do I need to legally rent out a villa in Bali?

At minimum, an NIB business registration with the correct KBLI accommodation code, NPWP tax registration, and a local tourism licence (commonly pondok wisata or an equivalent category depending on property type and regency). Requirements can vary slightly by area, so confirm locally.

### How long does it take to get all the permits for a villa in Bali?

It depends on the regency, the completeness of your documents, and current administrative processing times, and it is rarely a single-week process (as of 2026, verify current timelines with a licensed adviser). Having documents ready in advance is the biggest factor in shortening it.

### Can I apply for a local tourism licence without an NIB first?

Generally the tourism licence application expects the business registration to already exist, since it is usually a prerequisite document. Applying out of order tends to cause delays rather than speed things up.

### What documents do I need to start the permit process?

Proof of land or leasehold rights, building documentation matching the actual structure, identity documents for the registering individual or company, and confirmation of the land's zoning classification. Gather these before starting any application.

### Can a management company handle permits for me?

Many owners fold permit coordination into a management arrangement, particularly if they are based abroad, since the local tourism licence step usually needs someone physically present to liaise with the regency office. Confirm this is explicitly part of the service before assuming it is included.
