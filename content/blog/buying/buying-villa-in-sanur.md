A villa for sale in Sanur typically appeals to buyers looking for a quieter, more established east-coast area, calm sea suited to families, a long-standing expat community, and a mix of long-stay residents and holidaymakers rather than the fast-paced short-stay nightlife scene found elsewhere on the island. Sanur has seen steadier, more measured construction than some faster-growing areas, which has kept its market character consistent over time.

We manage 11 villas in Sanur ourselves, more than in any other area we operate in, and it is where we see the broadest range of buyer types: families wanting a long-term base, investors drawn to steady rental demand, and buyers who simply prefer the area's calmer pace to Bali's busier tourist hubs.

## What kind of villas are typically for sale in Sanur

Sanur's villa stock ranges from compact two-bedroom pool townhouses to larger four-bedroom family villas, often with generous garden space and, in several cases, proximity to Sanur's popular beach path. Our own Sanur listings include villas such as Villa Black Pool, a four-bedroom family villa with a large black-tiled pool, and the Sanur Garden Pool Villa, a four-bedroom property with a 35 square metre pool near Mertasari Beach, alongside smaller two and three-bedroom townhouse-style villas.

## What drives price within Sanur

Proximity to the beach path and Mertasari Beach, land and building size, leasehold term remaining, and finish quality all move price within the area, similar to the general drivers covered in our guide on what a villa in Bali actually costs. Sanur's steadier demand profile means price movements here tend to be less volatile than in faster-growing, more speculative segments elsewhere.

## Rental demand character in Sanur

Sanur attracts a mix of holidaymakers seeking a calmer stay, families, and longer-term residents on monthly or yearly leases, which gives villas here more diversified demand than areas relying heavily on short-stay nightly bookings alone. This diversification is one reason Sanur has been less exposed to the oversupply patterns covered in our guide on villa oversupply in Bali.

## What to check before buying in Sanur

1. Confirm the leasehold term and any extension provisions clearly
2. Check proximity to the beach path and key amenities, which materially affects both value and rental appeal
3. Ask for rental history if the villa has been managed or rented previously
4. Verify permits (PKKPR, PBG, SLF) are in place and match the property as built
5. Compare against genuinely comparable recent Sanur sales, not other areas

## Sanur's neighbourhoods worth knowing

Sanur is not a single uniform strip; villas closer to the beach path and Mertasari Beach tend to command a premium and attract more holiday and short-stay demand, while villas set back in the quieter residential lanes further from the coast often suit long-stay residents and families looking for a calmer, more affordable base with easy access to the beach by bike or a short drive. Knowing which micro-location fits your goals, rental income, personal use, or a mix of both, is worth working through before you start comparing specific listings.

## Financing and paperwork specific to a Sanur purchase

The paperwork involved in buying a villa in Sanur follows the same structure as anywhere else in Bali, leasehold agreement, land certificate, permits, sale-purchase agreement, notary and PPAT process, but it is still worth confirming area-specific details, such as whether the villa sits within any zoning restrictions particular to its street or block. ## FAQ

### Is Sanur a good area to buy a villa in Bali?
Sanur suits buyers looking for a calmer, more established area with diversified rental demand, families, long-stay residents and holidaymakers, rather than the faster-paced short-stay market found elsewhere. Whether it is "good" depends on your specific goals and budget.

### How does Sanur compare to Canggu for buying a villa?
Sanur is generally quieter and more family-oriented with steadier, more diversified demand, while Canggu has faster-growing, short-stay-heavy demand and has seen more rapid new construction. Each suits different buyer priorities and risk appetites.

### What size villas are typically available for sale in Sanur?
Sanur's villa stock spans from compact two-bedroom pool townhouses to larger four-bedroom family villas, often with generous garden space, giving buyers a range of options across different budgets and household sizes.

### Are Sanur villas good for rental income?
Sanur's diversified demand base, holidaymakers, families and longer-term residents, supports steadier rental demand than areas relying heavily on short-stay nightly bookings alone, though actual performance depends on the specific villa, its condition and how it is managed.

### Do I need to check anything specific to Sanur before buying?
Proximity to the beach path and key amenities affects value and rental appeal meaningfully within Sanur, alongside the standard checks, leasehold term, permits, rental history, that apply to any Bali villa purchase.
