Flights to Bali arrive at Ngurah Rai International Airport near Denpasar, and for most travellers outside South-East Asia and Australia, this means at least one connection through a regional hub. The routing and connection choice matters more than travellers sometimes expect, both for total journey time and for how you should plan your first night once you land.

## Direct vs connecting routes

Depending on where you're flying from, you may have a genuinely direct option or you may be routing through a hub city with a layover of anywhere from an hour to overnight. A longer layover can be an opportunity (a stopover city worth a day) or simply dead time, depending on how it's timed against your onward flight and how tired you'll be on arrival in Bali.

For families or anyone travelling with young children, minimising the total number of connections tends to matter more than shaving an hour off total flight time — a single reasonable connection is usually an easier trip than two shorter ones with tight transfer windows.

## Booking with a villa holiday in mind

If you're planning what's sometimes searched as a "Bali villa holiday with flights" — effectively booking flights and accommodation as a coordinated trip rather than separately — the practical approach is to book flights first (since availability and price move faster than most villa calendars), then confirm your villa dates once flight times are locked in, adjusting for arrival time. Booking direct with a villa manager rather than through a bundled package also means your accommodation cost is transparent and negotiable directly, without a platform's added margin.

## Timing your arrival against check-in

Villa check-in is typically from mid-afternoon, and if your flight lands early in the day, you'll usually have a gap before check-in — worth planning for, whether that's a slow lunch near the airport, an early check-in request (often possible outside high season, worth asking in advance), or simply factoring the wait into your first-day plans rather than being caught off guard by it.

| Arrival time | Typical plan |
|---|---|
| Early morning | Ask about early check-in, or plan a relaxed first few hours before check-in time |
| Afternoon | Usually aligns naturally with standard check-in |
| Evening/late | Confirm late arrival details with your villa in advance, especially for airport transfer timing |

## Family holiday packages vs booking direct

Bundled "family holiday package" style bookings can be convenient for a first-time visitor, but they typically carry a margin built into the price for the package operator, on top of whatever the airline and accommodation actually cost. Booking flights and a villa separately — direct with the villa manager, with no OTA-style fee on the accommodation side — often works out better value for a family that's comfortable planning a few extra details themselves.

## What to check before booking flights

- Total journey time including layovers, not just flight time
- Arrival time relative to villa check-in (typically from 3pm)
- Whether your chosen area (Sanur, Canggu, Umalas, Ungasan) suits your planned activities and travel style
- Airport transfer arrangements for your specific arrival time — see our [airport transfer cost guide](/blog/travel/bali-airport-transfer-cost/)
