The Indonesia arrival card is a customs and immigration declaration every traveller entering the country needs to complete, separate from whatever visa you're travelling on. It asks for standard details — passport information, accommodation address, length of stay — plus a customs declaration about what you're bringing in. Get the format and timing right and it takes a couple of minutes; get it wrong and it can mean an extra queue at the airport.

## Arrival card vs visa: two different things

This is the most common confusion we hear from villa guests. Your visa (see our guide to [Bali visa on arrival](/blog/travel/bali-visa-on-arrival/)) is your legal permission to enter and stay in Indonesia. The arrival card is a separate declaration about your specific trip — where you're staying, how long, and what you're carrying — required regardless of which visa type you're travelling on. You'll typically need to deal with both, usually in the same immigration process, but they're not interchangeable and completing one doesn't cover the other.

## How it's completed today

Indonesia has moved toward an online arrival card system for most travellers, completed shortly before arrival rather than on a paper form handed out on the plane. Exactly how far in advance you can or should submit it, and whether a paper fallback still exists at all entry points, is worth checking close to your travel date, since this process has changed more than once in recent years (as of 2026, verify). The authoritative source is the official Indonesian immigration site, imigrasi.go.id.

| What you'll typically need | Why |
|---|---|
| Passport details | Standard identity verification |
| Address of first accommodation | Where you're staying — have your villa address ready |
| Length of stay | Matches your visa/return flight |
| Customs declaration | What you're bringing into the country |
| Flight details | Arrival flight number and date |

## The customs declaration part

Beyond the immigration details, the arrival card includes a customs declaration covering items like cash above a certain threshold, goods for commercial purposes, and restricted items (certain foods, plants, animal products). The exact declaration thresholds and prohibited-item list are set by Indonesian customs and are worth checking directly rather than guessing, particularly if you're carrying anything unusual — medication in larger quantities, professional equipment, or amounts of cash above what feels like a normal travel amount (as of 2026, verify).

## When to fill it in

If using the online system, there's typically a window before arrival within which you're expected to submit it — not so early that your details might change, not so late that you're doing it in the immigration queue. Check the current guidance on imigrasi.go.id for the exact window, and consider completing it during your layover or shortly before landing rather than leaving it until you're queuing.

## What happens if you don't complete it

Arriving without a completed arrival card generally means completing it at the airport before you can clear immigration, which adds time to an already long post-flight process. For villa guests arranging an [airport transfer](/blog/travel/bali-airport-transfer-cost/), this is worth building into your expected clearance time so your driver isn't waiting unnecessarily and you're not stressed about the wait.

## FAQ

### Is the Indonesia arrival card the same as a visa application?
No. They're separate requirements — your visa is legal permission to enter and stay, while the arrival card is a customs and immigration declaration about your specific trip, required regardless of visa type.

### Do I complete the arrival card online or on paper?
Indonesia has moved toward an online system for most travellers, completed shortly before arrival, though the exact process and any paper fallback should be checked close to your travel date on imigrasi.go.id, since this has changed in recent years (as of 2026, verify).

### What address should I put on the arrival card?
Your first accommodation address in Indonesia — for villa guests, this is your villa's exact address, which we include in your booking confirmation. Have it saved and accessible before you start the form.

### What happens if I don't fill in the arrival card before landing?
You'll typically need to complete it at the airport before clearing immigration, which adds time to your arrival process. It's worth completing it during your layover or shortly before landing instead.

### Do children need their own arrival card?
Requirements for minors travelling with family can differ from the standard process, so check current guidance on imigrasi.go.id if you're travelling with children, rather than assuming one form covers the whole family (as of 2026, verify).
