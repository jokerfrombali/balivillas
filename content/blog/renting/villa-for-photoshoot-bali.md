Booking a villa as a photoshoot location in Bali is different from booking one to stay in. Most owners treat it as a separate, shorter arrangement — a few hours or a single day — rather than an overnight stay, and it comes with its own rules about crew size, equipment and which parts of the property a shoot can actually use.

If you're organising a shoot, the details that matter aren't in the listing photos: whether the owner allows commercial photography at all, how many people can be on-site, and what happens if the shoot overruns.

## Day-use versus overnight: two different bookings

A villa rented for a shoot is usually priced as day-use — a set number of hours, commonly a morning or afternoon block — separate from the overnight rate. If your team also wants to stay the night before or after to set up or wind down, that's typically booked and charged as a standard overnight stay on top of the day-use fee, not folded into one number automatically.

Ask specifically whether the quote you're given is hours-based or a full day, and what the overrun policy is if the shoot runs long — some owners charge a flat late fee, others simply won't allow it because the next guest is checking in.

## What "commercial use" means to an owner

Not every villa allows commercial photography, even if personal holiday photos are obviously fine. Weddings, product shoots, fashion editorials and influencer content are treated differently by different owners — some are happy to host any of it, others restrict to non-commercial personal use only. Always state clearly what the shoot is for when asking about availability; a vague "can we take photos" gets a vague answer, while "we're shooting a product campaign with a crew of six for four hours" gets a specific yes or no.

## Crew size and equipment

Photography and video crews bring more people and more gear than a typical guest group — lighting stands, reflectors, sometimes a small generator or drone. Confirm the villa's crew-size limit before booking, and ask specifically about drone use, since some compounds restrict it for neighbour-privacy reasons regardless of Indonesian airspace rules.

Checklist before locking a shoot date:

- Does the owner allow commercial photography/video, and does the type of shoot matter?
- What's the maximum crew and equipment on-site at once?
- Is drone use allowed, and are there restrictions from neighbouring properties?
- Which areas are off-limits — private owner storage, staff quarters, a neighbour-shared pool?
- What happens if the shoot needs extra time on the day?

## Light, timing and the practical side of a Bali shoot

Bali's tropical light is intense at midday and beautiful in the hour after sunrise and before sunset — plan the booking window around that rather than a generic "full day." Afternoon rain is common in the wet season (roughly November to March) and can shut down an outdoor shoot with little warning, so build a backup indoor plan or a flexible reschedule clause into your booking if timing is tight.

What typically affects the quote: the day-use rate for the villa and area, whether commercial use carries a premium over personal use, crew size beyond a base threshold, and any deposit for equipment risk to the pool, garden or furniture.

## FAQ

### Do all villas in Bali allow paid photoshoots?

No. Some owners restrict use to personal, non-commercial photos only. Always state the purpose of the shoot clearly when asking about availability so you get an accurate yes or no rather than assuming.

### Is a photoshoot booking priced differently from an overnight stay?

Usually, yes — shoots are commonly priced as day-use for a set number of hours, separate from the standard overnight rate. If your crew also needs to stay overnight, that's typically an additional booking.

### Can we fly a drone during a villa photoshoot?

Sometimes, but not always — some compounds restrict drone use for neighbour-privacy reasons independent of general Indonesian airspace rules. Confirm with the villa manager before planning a drone shot into the schedule.

### What happens if a shoot runs longer than booked?

Policies vary by owner — some allow a flat late fee, others can't extend because of the next guest's check-in. Ask about the overrun policy before the shoot day so there's no dispute on-site.

### Which area is best for a natural, editorial-style shoot?

Ungasan's teak clifftop villas suit earthy, natural editorial looks; Sanur gives softer beachside light; Canggu's larger villas suit shoots needing several backdrops in one location. The right choice depends on the visual brief.
