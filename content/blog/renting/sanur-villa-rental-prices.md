Sanur villa rental price depends mainly on bedroom count, how close the villa sits to the beach path, and how long you're staying, in that order of importance. Sanur is generally more competitively priced than beachfront Canggu for a comparable villa, which is part of why it's popular for families and longer stays, but the range within Sanur itself is still wide.

We manage 11 villas in Sanur, from 2-bedroom pool townhouses to 4-bedroom family houses, and quote each one directly based on dates rather than a fixed published rate. Here's what actually moves the number and what to check in any quote you receive.

## What drives villa rental in Sanur pricing

Bedroom count is the clearest driver — a 2-bedroom townhouse like our Stylish or Modern Pool Townhouse sits at a different price point from a 4-bedroom house like the Sanur Garden Pool Villa near Mertasari Beach. Distance to the beach path matters next, though less dramatically than in Canggu, since most of Sanur's villas sit on similarly quiet residential lanes a short walk or scooter ride from the water. Stay length is the third factor and arguably the most useful lever for a guest to control: nightly rates for a short stay differ substantially from what you'd pay per night on a monthly booking.

## Nightly, weekly and monthly rates compared

For a sanur villa monthly rental, the per-night cost typically drops well below the nightly-stay rate, since we apply a direct-booking discount of 15% for stays of a month or longer, on top of the lower effective daily cost that comes with longer bookings generally. Weekly stays get a 10% discount. Short stays under a week are priced at the standard nightly rate, with a 5% direct-booking discount applied regardless of length.

| Stay length | Discount off direct rate | Typical guest |
|---|---|---|
| Under 1 week | 5% | Short holiday |
| 1 week+ | 10% | Extended holiday |

## What's included in a Sanur villa rental price

Across our Sanur villas, the rate typically covers housekeeping, Wi-Fi and full use of the kitchen and pool. What can vary is cleaning frequency on longer stays, whether airport transfer is included, and whether extra guests beyond standard capacity carry a surcharge. Ask these directly rather than assuming they're bundled, since practices differ by villa and length of stay.

- Confirm whether the quote is per night, per week, or a monthly all-in rate
- Ask about housekeeping frequency, especially for stays of a month or longer
- Check if airport transfer is included or arranged separately
- Confirm extra-guest policy if your group might exceed standard capacity

## What tends to catch first-time bookers out

Two things surprise guests comparing sanur villa rental price options for the first time. The first is that a villa's proximity to Mertasari Beach specifically, rather than the beach path generally, can carry a small premium, since that stretch is popular for its calm swimming conditions and morning walks. The second is that extra-guest policies vary: some villas charge a supplement once you exceed the standard occupancy for a given bedroom count, even if there's a sofa bed that technically fits more people, so confirm this before assuming a villa can flex to a larger group at no extra cost.

## How Sanur's off-season compares

April–June and September–November tend to be Sanur's best value windows: fewer crowds, comparable weather to peak season, and villa rental in sanur pricing that's typically a step below the July–August and December–January peaks. If your dates are flexible, shifting a trip by even a few weeks either side of peak season can noticeably change the quote you receive, without much trade-off in weather, since Sanur rarely gets extreme conditions even in the wetter months.

- Ask us directly whether your dates fall inside or outside peak pricing
- If flexible, request quotes for two or three date windows to compare
- Confirm extra-guest terms before assuming a villa flexes beyond its standard occupancy
