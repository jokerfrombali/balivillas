Canggu villa rental price is set mainly by three things: how many bedrooms you need, how close the villa is to the beach or Canggu's café strip, and what season you're travelling in. A 2-bedroom villa inland and a 9-bedroom resort-style villa near the beach are both "Canggu villas," but their rates aren't remotely comparable, so the useful question isn't "what does a Canggu villa cost" but "what does a villa matching my group size and location preference cost."

We manage two villas in Canggu and quote directly rather than publishing a fixed rate card, because availability, length of stay and season all move the number. This guide explains what actually drives the price so you can judge whether a quote you receive is reasonable.

## The main factors behind any quote

Bedroom count is the biggest driver: a 2-bedroom townhouse and a 6-bedroom beachside villa aren't priced on the same scale, since land, staffing and pool size all scale up together. Proximity to the beach matters next — our villa The Wave sits a 2–5 minute walk from Berawa Beach, and that kind of walking distance typically carries a premium over an equivalent villa 15–20 minutes away by scooter. Season is the third major factor: July–August and the December–January holiday period see higher demand and higher rates across Bali, while the shoulder months (April–June, September–November) tend to offer better value for a comparable villa.

## What's typically included and what costs extra

What varies villa to villa is extras like airport transfers, a private chef, or additional cleaning during a longer stay — always confirm what's included in the quoted rate rather than assuming, since practices differ between managers.

- Ask about extra-guest charges if your group exceeds the villa's standard capacity

## What tends to surprise first-time bookers

Two things catch people out most often. First, weekend and holiday pricing can differ noticeably from weekday rates for the same villa, so a quote for a Friday–Sunday stay may look different from a midweek one even at the same villa. Second, larger villas (6+ bedrooms) often represent better value per person than expected, because the fixed costs of running a villa (pool, garden, staff) are spread across more guests — worth factoring in if you're comparing a large villa split between families against several smaller bookings.

## How group size changes the value equation

For a group deciding between two smaller villas or one larger one, canggu villa rental price comparisons should always be run per person, not per villa. A 6-bedroom villa like The Wave, split across 12 guests, frequently works out cheaper per head than booking two 3-bedroom villas separately, since a single set of pool, garden and housekeeping costs is being spread across more people.
