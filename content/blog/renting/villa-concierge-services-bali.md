Villa concierge services in Bali typically means asking your villa manager to arrange things beyond the property itself, a driver, a private chef, spa treatments, excursions, rather than booking a separate, dedicated concierge company. Most self-managed villa operations, including ours, will help arrange these directly or point you to trusted local providers, since we're already in touch with you over WhatsApp for the booking itself.

The key is asking early. Extras like a private chef or a driver for a specific day are much easier to arrange with a few days' notice than on the morning you want them.

## What villa managers can typically help arrange

| Request | How it usually works |
|---|---|
| Private chef | Arranged ahead, usually a few days' notice for the best availability |
| Car and driver | Can often be arranged for airport transfer or day trips |
| Massage or spa at the villa | Booked in advance for a specific time |
| Grocery delivery | Arranged before or during your stay |
| Babysitting | Arranged with notice, confirm details directly |
| Excursions (day trips, tours) | Managers can usually point you to trusted local operators |

## When to ask for concierge-style extras

Ask as early as possible, ideally when you first book, especially for a private chef, a specific driver for the whole stay, or anything during a busy period like July–August or Christmas–New Year when local providers are in higher demand too. Requests made the day you want them are harder to guarantee than ones planned a few days ahead.

## Concierge extras versus what's included by default

Don't assume any of these are included in your villa rate by default; housekeeping, linen and Wi-Fi are typically standard, everything else (chef, driver, spa) is arranged and charged separately. ## What's realistic to expect from a villa manager versus a dedicated concierge

It's worth being clear-eyed about the difference between a villa management company helping arrange a few extras and a dedicated luxury concierge service whose entire business is curating experiences. We fall into the first category, we know our villas and our local area well, and we're happy to help arrange a driver, a chef or a massage because we're already in touch with you. But we're not set up to plan a multi-day itinerary of activities, arrange event-level catering, or manage complex logistics across the island the way a specialist concierge company would.

If your trip needs that level of planning, a wedding, a large corporate retreat, an elaborate multi-stop itinerary, it's worth engaging a dedicated planner alongside your villa booking rather than expecting a villa manager to cover both roles. For most stays, though, the extras guests actually ask for (transport, a chef for a night or two, a massage, help with a day trip) are well within what a good local villa manager can arrange directly or point you toward.

## Timing extras around your check-in and check-out

Extras are easiest to arrange when they're planned around your actual schedule rather than requested on the day. If you want a chef for your first night, let us know before you land so groceries and preparation are ready when you arrive at 3:00 pm. If you want a driver for the airport, request this well ahead so it's confirmed rather than arranged in a rush after you land. Building your extras into the booking conversation from the start, rather than treating them as afterthoughts, is the simplest way to make sure everything runs smoothly.

## Special occasions and celebrations

If your stay includes a specific celebration, an anniversary, a birthday, a honeymoon, tell us when you book rather than mentioning it on arrival. A small setup (flowers, a decorated dinner table, a cake arranged ahead of time) is often straightforward to arrange with a few days' notice but much harder to put together on short notice once you're already in the villa. This is one of the more rewarding parts of arranging extras through a local manager, since we can often suggest something fitting based on what's realistically available locally, rather than you needing to research and coordinate it all yourself from abroad.

## A quick checklist for arranging extras

- Ask for concierge-style extras when you first book, not on arrival.
- Confirm cost for each extra separately, since these aren't included in the villa rate.
- For a private chef, mention dietary requirements and group size upfront.
- During peak season, book drivers and chefs further ahead given higher local demand.
- Ask your manager directly rather than assuming a service exists without confirming.

## FAQ

### Do Bali villas come with a concierge service included?

Not as a separate bundled service, most villa managers, including us, help arrange extras like a private chef, driver or spa treatment directly through the same conversation you use to book the villa. These are arranged and charged separately from the villa rate itself, not included by default.

### How far ahead should I book a private chef for my Bali villa?

Ideally a few days ahead, especially during busier periods like July–August or the Christmas–New Year weeks when local chefs and drivers are in higher demand across all areas. Mention dietary requirements and group size when you ask so the arrangement can be confirmed properly.

### Can a villa manager arrange excursions and day trips in Bali?

Often yes, or they can point you to trusted local operators for tours and day trips, even if they don't run the excursion themselves. Ask directly what's possible for the specific day trip you have in mind; local knowledge from a manager who's based in the area is often more useful than a generic listing.

### Is it cheaper to arrange a driver through the villa or book separately?

This varies, and it's worth asking your manager what they'd charge or recommend versus checking independently. The advantage of asking through the villa is convenience and a trusted local connection, since we're already in contact with you for the booking itself.

### What concierge extras are most commonly requested for Bali villa stays?

Based on what we see, airport transfer and a car and driver for the stay are the most common requests, followed by a private chef for a special occasion and massage or spa treatments at the villa. Grocery delivery ahead of arrival is also popular, particularly for families with young children.
