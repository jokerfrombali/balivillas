Almost every villa in Bali treats indoor smoking the same way: not allowed. Outdoor space — a terrace, a garden, poolside seating — is where guests are expected to smoke instead, and most villas are built with exactly that setup in mind: open-air living areas, covered outdoor lounges, and bedrooms kept smoke-free.

This isn't a Bali-specific quirk. It's standard for private villa rentals worldwide, driven by fire risk on wooden and thatched structures, soft furnishings that hold smoke smell for weeks, and the cost of repainting or deep-cleaning a room afterwards. Knowing this before you book saves an awkward conversation on arrival.

## Why indoor smoking is treated so strictly

Bali villas often use natural materials — teak, alang-alang thatch roofing, rattan furniture, cotton soft furnishings — that absorb smoke smell far more readily than a hotel room with sealed surfaces and mechanical ventilation. A single indoor smoking session can leave a smell that lingers through several guest turnovers, and removing it usually means professional cleaning, sometimes repainting. That cost gets passed on, which is why villas price it as a cleaning fee rather than just asking politely.

Fire risk is the other half of it. Many villas have open eaves, dry season landscaping and, in some cases, thatched roofing, so an indoor cigarette isn't only about smell — it's a real hazard the manager has to account for.

## Where you can actually smoke

Outdoor terraces, garden areas and poolside loungers are the normal, expected place. Most of our villas are designed around indoor-outdoor living specifically so this isn't a restriction that cuts off the whole property — you're not confined to a small balcony. Bedrooms, indoor lounges and enclosed kitchens are the areas to avoid.

Vaping generally falls under the same rule as smoking indoors, even though it doesn't leave the same lingering smell — check with the specific villa if you're unsure, since policies aren't always written down for vapes separately from cigarettes.

## Cigarette availability and local brands

Cigarettes are widely available at convenience stores (minimarkets), warungs and supermarkets across all four of our areas, so bringing a large supply from home is rarely necessary. Local and international brands are both common, and prices are generally lower than in many Western countries, though this varies by brand. If you vape, bringing your own device and preferred liquid is more reliable, since specialist vape shops are concentrated in Canggu and less common in quieter areas like Sanur and Ungasan.

## What happens if you smoke inside anyway

| Situation | Typical outcome |
|---|---|
| Smoked once outdoors on terrace/garden | No issue, expected use of the space |
| Smoked indoors, smell detected at checkout | Cleaning fee charged, amount varies by villa |
| Repeated indoor smoking during stay | Warning from housekeeping or management, possible added fee |
| Burn mark or damage from smoking | Charged as damage, separate from the cleaning fee |
| Vaping indoors | Treated the same as smoking in most villas |

Exact cleaning-fee amounts vary by villa and aren't something we publish as a fixed figure — ask when you book if it matters to you, and we'll give you a straight answer for the specific property.

## A short checklist before you light up

- Check whether the villa has a stated outdoor smoking area in its house rules or ask on arrival.
- Keep cigarettes, lighters and ashtrays away from bedrooms and indoor lounges.
- Use an ashtray outdoors rather than the ground, particularly during dry season.
- If you vape, confirm with the villa whether it's treated the same as smoking.
- Air out any indoor space promptly if smoke drifts in from outside, rather than leaving it.

## Smoking policies at restaurants, cafés and public venues nearby

Beyond the villa itself, Bali's wider hospitality scene generally follows a similar pattern to villas: indoor dining areas at most restaurants and cafés are non-smoking, with outdoor seating sections available for smokers. Beach clubs and open-air venues tend to be more relaxed given their largely outdoor layout. This is useful context if smoking matters to how you plan your evenings out, not just where you stay, since the same indoor-outdoor split you will find at your villa tends to hold across most tourist-oriented venues in Sanur, Canggu, Umalas and Ungasan.

## Non-smoking guests sharing a villa

If you are travelling with someone who does not smoke, or booking a family villa where a non-smoking environment matters, it helps to mention this alongside any smoking-related question when you enquire. Most of our villas naturally separate indoor and outdoor living well enough that a non-smoking guest sharing the property with someone who smokes outdoors rarely notices any smell indoors, but if you want extra reassurance, ask specifically how far the nearest outdoor seating area is from the main bedroom windows.

## Designated smoking corners versus general outdoor space

Some larger villas, particularly ones with multiple bedroom blocks or a shared compound, mark out a specific smoking corner rather than leaving the whole outdoor area open to it, usually to keep smoke away from a shared pool deck or dining table where other guests might be eating. Smaller, standalone villas rented by a single group rarely need this distinction, since the whole outdoor space belongs to that one booking. If you are booking a larger property or one shared with other households, it is worth asking whether there is a specific outdoor smoking spot rather than assuming the entire terrace is fair game.

## What guests who smoke tell us works best

Guests who smoke regularly and want a stress-free stay tend to do two things: they mention it briefly when booking, so the host can point out the best outdoor spot on arrival, and they bring their own small portable ashtray for evenings by the pool where a fixed ashtray might not be within reach. Neither is required, but both save a small amount of friction, particularly during a longer stay where the habit becomes part of the daily routine rather than an occasional thing.

## Cigarette disposal and the local environment

Bali's waste management infrastructure varies by area, and discarded cigarette butts in gardens or pool surrounds are one of the more common minor complaints villa staff deal with after a group stay. Using an ashtray consistently, rather than flicking a butt into a garden bed or pool, is a small courtesy that also avoids a mention on the final cleaning report. Most villas provide ashtrays in the outdoor seating areas precisely to make this easy.

## FAQ

### Can I smoke on the balcony or terrace of a Bali villa?

Yes, in almost every case. Outdoor terraces, gardens and poolside seating are the expected place to smoke, and most villas are designed with generous outdoor living space for exactly this reason. Indoor rooms — bedrooms, enclosed lounges, kitchens — are where the restriction applies. If you're unsure about a specific property, ask before booking rather than assuming.

### Will I be charged if I smoke inside a Bali villa?

Likely, if the smell is detected at checkout. Cleaning fees for indoor smoking vary by villa and aren't usually published as a fixed number, since the cost depends on how much cleaning or repainting is needed. If it's a one-off with minimal smell, some villas let it go with a warning; repeated indoor smoking is more likely to result in a charge.

### Are e-cigarettes and vapes allowed inside Bali villas?

Most villas apply the same indoor restriction to vaping as to smoking, even though vape vapour doesn't linger the same way cigarette smoke does. It's not always written into house rules as a separate line, so if you vape regularly during your stay, it's worth confirming directly with the villa rather than assuming it's treated differently from smoking.

### Do all villas in Bali have an outdoor smoking area?

Nearly all do, since indoor-outdoor living is the default layout for private pool villas here — terraces, gardens and poolside lounges are standard, not an add-on. A small handful of enclosed, apartment-style units have less outdoor space, so if outdoor smoking matters to you, check the layout of the specific villa rather than assuming every property has generous terrace space.

### Is smoking marijuana or other drugs different from tobacco in a villa?

Yes, and this isn't a house-rules matter — Indonesian law treats drug possession and use as a criminal offence with serious penalties, regardless of what a villa's smoking policy says (as of 2026, verify). Villa house rules only cover tobacco and vaping. Nothing in this guide applies to illegal substances, and we don't advise on that separately from the law itself.
