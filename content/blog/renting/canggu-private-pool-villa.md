Canggu is where most people picture Bali villa life: surf breaks, beach clubs, a private pool a few steps from the sunbed. A genuine Canggu private pool villa is not hard to find, but "private pool" covers a wide range of setups, from a 2x2m plunge pool on a rooftop to a full 8-metre pool with a garden. Knowing which one you are booking, and where in Canggu it sits, matters more than the marketing photos.

We manage two villas in Canggu ourselves, so this is written from the operations side: what guests ask us before booking, what goes wrong when they don't ask, and what actually varies from one Canggu private pool villa to the next.

## What "private pool" means in practice

In Canggu listings, "private pool" can mean three different things. First, a pool used only by your booking, fully enclosed within your villa's walls — the strongest version and what most guests assume they are getting. Second, a pool shared between two or three villas on the same small compound, where "private" means private to the compound, not to you. Third, a rooftop or courtyard plunge pool too small to swim laps in but still exclusively yours.

None of these is wrong to book, but they suit different trips. A family with young children wants full enclosure and shallow-enough depth, not a rooftop plunge pool six floors up. A couple on a short break might not care whether the pool is 3 metres or 8. Ask the manager directly: is the pool used only by our group, and what are its dimensions and depth. If a listing doesn't answer that in the first message, that is itself useful information.

## How location inside Canggu changes the villa

Canggu is not one neighbourhood. Berawa, near the beach clubs and Finns, puts you a short walk or scooter ride from the surf and the busiest cafés, but it is noisier and more built-up than it was a few years ago. Pererenan and Batu Bolong, further from the main strip, trade some convenience for quieter streets and lower prices on comparable villas. Traffic on the main Canggu roads at peak times (roughly 4pm–7pm) can add real minutes to any trip, so a villa's walking distance to the beach or to a café cluster matters more here than in most parts of Bali.

Our villa The Wave sits a 2–5 minute walk from Berawa Beach, which is the kind of distance worth confirming for any Canggu private pool villa you are comparing — "5 minutes to the beach" and "15 minutes by scooter" get sold with the same phrase sometimes.

## A short checklist before you commit

- Confirm the pool is enclosed and private to your group, not shared with a neighbouring villa
- Ask for pool dimensions and depth if you have children or want to swim proper laps
- Check the actual walking distance to the beach or the café strip you want, not just "Canggu"
- Ask what's included: pool cleaning schedule, towels, and whether the pool is heated (most Bali villa pools are not, and don't need to be)
- Confirm check-in (3:00 pm) and check-out (11:00 am) times, and ask about early check-in if you land in the morning

## What goes wrong when guests don't check first

The most common disappointment we hear about from guests who booked elsewhere isn't the villa itself, it's a mismatch between expectation and reality: a "private pool" that turns out to be shared, a "5-minute walk to the beach" that's actually a 15-minute walk along a busy road, or a villa photographed with a wide-angle lens that makes a small pool look large. None of this is necessarily dishonest, but it means the burden is on you to ask specific questions before booking, not to trust the headline description.

The second most common issue is around group size versus bedroom count. A villa listed for "8 guests" across 4 bedrooms might mean two people per room with no sofa bed, or it might include a sofa bed that changes the actual comfortable capacity. If you're travelling as a group, ask for bed configuration, not just guest capacity.

## Seasonal factors that affect a Canggu stay

Bali's dry season (roughly April to October) is the most popular time to book a villa canggu bali private pool stay, with the driest, sunniest stretch usually falling between June and September. The wet season (November to March) still sees plenty of sun, just with more frequent short afternoon showers, and villa prices in Canggu often ease slightly outside July–August and the December–January holiday peak. A private pool matters more in the dry months when you're likely to use it daily; in wetter months, check that the villa has good covered living space too, since you may spend more time indoors between showers.

Canggu's surf season also affects who's around: peak surf months bring more visitors specifically for the waves, which can mean livelier beach clubs and busier cafés. If you want a quieter version of Canggu, ask us which weeks tend to be calmer.

## Getting around once you're there

Most guests get around Canggu by scooter, which is the fastest way to cover the area's spread-out cafés, beach breaks and shops, though it does require a valid licence and comfort riding on Bali's roads. A private driver or car is a common alternative for families or anyone not confident on a scooter, particularly useful for airport transfers and evenings out. Walking works for short distances near your villa but isn't practical for crossing Canggu given the distances and lack of continuous footpaths in places.

If you're booking a villa canggu private pool stay and don't plan to rent a scooter, factor transport cost and time into your comparison between a villa close to the beach and cafés versus one further out but cheaper — the savings on rent can disappear into daily transport costs if you're not careful.

## FAQ

### Are Canggu villas with private pool usually heated?
No, almost all villa pools in Canggu are unheated, which is fine year-round because Bali's air and water temperatures stay warm. Heating isn't standard and rarely needed; if a listing specifically advertises a heated pool, it's the exception rather than the norm, so don't assume one is included unless it's stated.

### How far in advance should I book a villa in Canggu with private pool?
For July–August and the December–January holiday period, book 2–3 months ahead if you want a specific villa, since good Canggu properties fill early. Outside those windows, 2–4 weeks is usually enough, and last-minute availability does happen, so it's worth asking even close to your travel dates.

### Can I get a villa canggu private pool option within walking distance of Berawa Beach?
Yes, some Canggu villas sit a 2–5 minute walk from Berawa Beach, our villa The Wave among them. Always confirm the exact distance rather than relying on "near the beach" in a listing, since that phrase gets used loosely across Canggu.

### Is it cheaper to book a private pool villa in Canggu direct or through Airbnb?
Direct booking usually avoids the platform's service fee, and we don't charge a booking fee through our own site. Prices otherwise track the same market rates; the main advantage of booking direct is faster, more flexible communication with the people actually running the villa.

### What's the difference between a shared and private pool villa in Canggu?
A private pool is enclosed within your villa and used only by your group. A shared pool serves two or more villas on the same compound, still often called "private" in listings because it's not a public pool. Always ask which type you're booking if pool exclusivity matters to your stay.
