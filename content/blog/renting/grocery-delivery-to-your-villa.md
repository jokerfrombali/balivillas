Grocery delivery to a villa in Bali runs almost entirely through ride-hailing and delivery apps rather than dedicated grocery services, and it works well once you know which app to use where. GoJek and Grab both cover supermarket and convenience-store delivery across Sanur, Canggu, Umalas and Ungasan, and both let you order from a specific store rather than a curated list, which matters if you have a preferred supermarket nearby.

The order arrives by scooter, usually within 30–60 minutes depending on distance and traffic, and payment is typically in-app or cash on delivery. For a villa stay, the main decision is less "which app" and more "how much do we stock up versus order little and often" — and that depends on how long you're staying and how much kitchen space and fridge capacity the villa has.

## The apps and what they actually cover

GoJek's GoMart and Grab's GrabMart are the two main options, both pulling from local supermarkets and minimarkets such as Pepito, Bintang, or the nearest Indomaret or Alfamart depending on the area. Coverage and delivery times vary by exact location — a villa in central Sanur or Umalas is typically better served than one further from the main roads. Neither app requires a local bank card; most international cards and e-wallets set up through the app work fine, though it is worth testing a small order first.

## Stocking up versus ordering as you go

| Approach | Works well when | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| Big stock-up on day one | Longer stays, villas with full-size fridge/freezer | Fresh produce doesn't keep in tropical heat; buy perishables in smaller batches |
| Little and often | Short stays, smaller kitchens, wanting variety | More delivery fees stacking up if ordered daily |
| Mixed approach | Most stays over a week | Staples (rice, pasta, coffee, snacks) upfront; fresh items every few days |

For most guests staying a week or more, a mixed approach works best: a bigger first order for pantry staples, drinking water, and household basics, then smaller top-ups for fresh produce, meat and dairy every few days.

## What a villa kitchen typically has already

Every villa we manage has a fully equipped kitchen — cookware, a stove or hob, a fridge, and basic utensils — but not food. Some also stock starter items like coffee, tea, cooking oil and salt, though this is not guaranteed across every property, so check the specific listing or ask before arrival if it matters to your plans. Drinking water is supplied or easily arranged separately from grocery delivery, since tap water is not drinkable anywhere in Bali. If you are still deciding what to bring yourself versus source locally, our [packing guide](/blog/renting/what-to-bring-to-a-bali-villa/) covers that split in more detail — this page is specifically about what you can order in once you've landed.

## Local markets and warungs as an alternative

Traditional markets (pasar) and local warungs are a cheaper and often fresher alternative to app delivery for produce, though they don't deliver, so you would need to go in person or ask a driver to pick items up. Many long-stay guests use a mix: app delivery for packaged goods and staples, a market trip or two for fresh produce, and the odd warung meal instead of cooking. This is also where a driver becomes genuinely useful rather than a novelty, since a market run is easier with transport arranged, something covered in our guide to [arranging a car and driver from your villa](/blog/renting/car-and-driver-from-your-villa/).

## A simple first-order checklist

- Drinking water (unless the villa already supplies a full dispenser)
- Pantry basics: rice, pasta, oil, coffee or tea, snacks
- Fresh essentials for two to three days only, not a full week
- Basic cleaning supplies if not already stocked
- Any specific dietary items that may be harder to find locally (certain imported brands, specialty flours, etc.)

## Dietary needs and specialty items

If you're vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free or managing a specific dietary requirement, larger supermarkets in Sanur, Canggu and Umalas generally stock a reasonable range of substitutes and imported goods, though selection is narrower than a big-city supermarket back home. It is worth searching the specific product on GoMart or GrabMart before your trip to confirm it's available near your villa, rather than assuming. Specialty health food stores exist in Canggu and Umalas in particular, though these usually operate on their own delivery or pickup arrangements rather than through the general grocery apps.

## Ordering for special occasions

If you're planning a celebration meal or a specific dish that needs harder-to-find ingredients, order a few days ahead where possible rather than same-day, since some specialty items are only available at larger supermarkets further from your villa and take longer to source and deliver. If you'd prefer not to cook at all for an occasion, arranging a private chef for the evening is a separate service from grocery delivery and worth asking your villa manager about directly rather than trying to coordinate it through a grocery app.

## What happens if an item is out of stock

Both GoMart and GrabMart typically let the shopper substitute or contact you if an item is unavailable, rather than simply cancelling that line. Confirm your in-app notification settings allow this contact, since missing a substitution prompt can mean an odd stand-in item shows up instead of what you wanted. For anything you're particular about, adding a note in the order ("no substitute, skip if unavailable") avoids this.

## Fresh produce, meat and seafood delivery

Beyond app-based supermarket delivery, some guests prefer sourcing fresh produce and seafood more directly, particularly in Sanur and Canggu where local fish markets and organic produce sellers operate. These generally don't integrate with GoMart or GrabMart, so arranging delivery means either a phone call and a driver pickup, or visiting in person. For a villa stay of any real length, it's worth asking your villa manager which of these exist near your specific property, since quality and reliability vary considerably between individual sellers in a way a generic app listing won't tell you.

## Alcohol and restricted items

Alcohol delivery through GoMart and GrabMart is inconsistent and depends on the specific store's licensing and the app's local policy, which can change without much notice. Larger supermarkets and dedicated bottle shops are usually a more reliable source if you want a specific selection, and some villa areas have a bottle shop within easy driving distance. If alcohol is a priority for your stay, it's worth checking availability through the app before you arrive rather than assuming a full range will show up in search results.

## Scaling delivery for a group or family stay

A larger group changes the grocery equation meaningfully: bigger first orders, more frequent top-ups, and more value in stocking a wider range of pantry basics upfront rather than ordering piecemeal. For a family or group staying in one of our larger villas, coordinating one shared shopping list rather than several individual small orders keeps delivery fees down and reduces the number of separate deliveries arriving through the day. It also helps to confirm fridge and freezer capacity before a big stock-up, since some villas have compact fridges better suited to frequent smaller orders than one large weekly shop.

## Costs to expect

Delivery fees through GoMart or GrabMart are typically small, often a modest flat fee plus distance, and prices for groceries themselves are broadly in line with or slightly above in-store prices at the same supermarket, since these are indicative platform markups rather than fixed rates. Minimarket items (Indomaret, Alfamart) tend to be cheaper than larger supermarkets for basics but have less selection for fresh produce or imported goods.

## FAQ

### Can I get groceries delivered to any villa in Bali?

Coverage is very good across Sanur, Canggu, Umalas and Ungasan through GoJek's GoMart and Grab's GrabMart, though delivery speed and exact store options depend on how close the villa is to a covered supermarket or minimarket. It is worth checking app coverage for the exact address once you have booked.

### Do villa kitchens come stocked with food?

No, not as standard. Villa kitchens are fully equipped with cookware and appliances, but food is not included unless a specific listing says otherwise. Some villas leave starter items like coffee, oil or salt, but guests should plan to order or buy groceries themselves for anything beyond that.

### Is it cheaper to order groceries or buy at a local market?

Local markets and warungs are usually cheaper for fresh produce than app-delivered supermarket groceries, but they require going in person since they don't deliver. Many guests combine both: app delivery for packaged staples and a market trip for fresh items, especially on a longer stay.

### How long does grocery delivery take to a Bali villa?

Typically 30 to 60 minutes through GoMart or GrabMart, depending on distance from the store and traffic conditions. Ordering earlier in the day generally means a faster delivery than during evening rush periods, particularly in busier areas like Canggu.

### What if my villa doesn't have great app delivery coverage?

Coverage gaps are more common in quieter lanes further from main roads. In that case, arranging a driver for a supermarket run is a reliable alternative, and it is worth asking your villa manager for the nearest well-stocked supermarket before you arrive.
