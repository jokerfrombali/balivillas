Questions owners ask

Villa management FAQ

How much can my villa earn in Bali?
It depends on area, size, condition and season — a realistic range only makes sense after a viewing. We’ll give you an honest estimate based on villas we already run in Sanur, Canggu, Umalas and Ungasan, not a number designed to win the contract.
What does management cost?
A single commission on actual bookings — nothing when the villa doesn’t earn. The exact rate depends on the villa and the scope of work; message us on WhatsApp and you’ll have our terms the same day.
Can I still stay in my own villa?
Of course. We block owner dates in the calendar whenever you want — many of our owners spend part of the year in their villa and rent it the rest of the time.
Who handles guests at 2am?
We do. Our team is in Bali, guest support runs 24/7, and owners are never woken up by a broken air-con or a late check-in.
My villa is already listed on Airbnb — what changes?
We take over the listings (or rebuild them properly), sync one calendar across all channels, add our direct-booking site on top and manage pricing per season. Existing future bookings are honoured — nothing is cancelled.
