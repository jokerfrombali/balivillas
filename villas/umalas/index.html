Villas in Umalas, Bali

Umalas is a practical choice for guests who want a quieter Bali base while staying close to Canggu, Berawa and Seminyak. The area works well for monthly stays, families, remote work and travelers who want more privacy than the busiest beach zones.

This page shows private villas for rent in Umalas and keeps the same filters as the main Bali rental catalog. You can narrow the list by bedrooms, monthly budget, amenities, private pool, kitchen, Wi-Fi and type of stay.

Why rent in Umalas?

Umalas gives quick access to cafés, gyms, beaches and international schools while feeling calmer than central Canggu or Seminyak.

Who is it for?

The area suits couples, families and remote workers looking for private pool villas, good road access and a longer-stay setup.

How to book?

Open a villa card, check the details and send a WhatsApp request. We can confirm availability, price and management options.
