Villas in Umalas, Bali

Umalas is the strip of rice fields and small lanes between Canggu and Seminyak — residential rather than touristy, and popular with people who stay for months rather than days. Quiet at night, with cafes, gyms and coworking a few minutes away.

This page shows private villas for rent in Umalas and keeps the same filters as the main Bali rental catalog. You can narrow the list by bedrooms, guests, amenities, private pool, kitchen, Wi-Fi and type of stay.

Why rent in Umalas?

Berawa and Canggu beaches are about 10 minutes by scooter, Seminyak and the Petitenget restaurants around 15, and Ngurah Rai airport roughly 30 minutes by car. A scooter is the practical way to get around.

Who is it for?

The area suits families, couples and remote workers looking for private pool villas, easy walking, beach access and a relaxed longer-stay setup.

How to book?

Open a villa card, check the details and send a WhatsApp request. We can confirm availability, price and management options.
