About the property

Two-storey 341 m² villa built in 2023, a 2–5 minute walk from Berawa Beach in Canggu. Six bedrooms, each with a king bed, a TV and its own en-suite bathroom, plus a separate guest toilet. Open-plan living room with a Smart TV and fully equipped kitchen opening onto a large private pool through full-height glass doors; a wide first-floor balcony overlooks the garden. Fast Wi-Fi, a safe in every room and housekeeping three times a week are included. Sleeps up to 12 — suited to large families, groups of friends, weddings and company retreats.
