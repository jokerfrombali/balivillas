House rules

Please treat the villa with care — it’s a private home, not a hotel.

  • No smoking indoors (terrace and pool area is okay)
  • No parties or loud gatherings
  • Quiet hours from 10 PM to 8 AM
  • No unregistered guests without prior approval
  • Please don’t eat or drink on the beds
  • Keep doors closed to prevent insects from entering
  • Turn off lights and A/C when leaving the villa — help us save energy 🌱
  • Respect the space, the neighbors, and the island 🌴
  • If you need anything — we’re always here to help via Airbnb or WA.

Other things to note

Please treat the villa as your own — we’ve done our best to create a comfortable, clean, and beautiful space for you to enjoy 🙏

• Smoking is not allowed indoors. On the terrace or by the pool — no problem.
• Ants and insects in Bali react quickly to uncovered food — please store everything properly.
• Kindly switch off lights and A/C when leaving the villa for an extended time — it helps save energy and supports a more eco-friendly stay.
• After swimming in the sea or pool, please avoid sitting on sofas or beds while wet.
• Quiet hours are from 10 PM to 8 AM — we kindly ask you to respect the peace of the neighborhood.
• Please avoid placing fruit or food directly on wooden surfaces — use a tray or plate.
• Cleaning is done on a fixed schedule. If you’d like to skip or reschedule, just let us know.
• We’re always nearby — feel free to message us on WA or Airbnb if you need anything at all.
