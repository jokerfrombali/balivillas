About the property

Premier Umalas 16 is a stylish 3-bedroom townhouse with a private pool in quiet Umalas, about 10 minutes to the beach by scooter. It combines modern tropical design, privacy and a practical layout for families, groups, digital nomads and longer stays.

The 140 m² townhouse spans two floors. Upstairs are three bright suite bedrooms, each with its own bathroom and balcony. Downstairs, the living room opens directly to the fully private pool. The home is fully air-conditioned and includes fast Wi-Fi, workspaces, Smart TV and a fully equipped kitchen.

Umalas is a calm base between Canggu, Berawa and Seminyak, with cafés, gyms and restaurants nearby while staying away from the busiest beach traffic.
