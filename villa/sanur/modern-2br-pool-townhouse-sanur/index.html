About this villa

A new 110 m² two-storey townhouse tucked into a quiet part of Sanur, about a 15-minute walk from the beach. It has a private entrance from a calm street and a fully enclosed pool and terrace that are entirely yours.

Downstairs, a bright living room with a Smart TV opens onto the pool terrace, alongside a fully equipped kitchen (microwave, blender, rice cooker, coffee machine, kettle) and a guest toilet. Upstairs are two bedrooms, each with a king-size bed and its own bathroom; one bedroom has a dedicated workspace.

High ceilings and large windows keep the house full of natural light, with air conditioning in every room and 300 Mbps Wi-Fi throughout. Housekeeping comes three times a week, with pool and garden care twice a week — comfortable for families, couples and long-term stays.
