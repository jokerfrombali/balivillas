About the property

Villa Black Pool is a tropical oasis offering a secluded retreat with resort-style luxury. This stunning estate features a private saltwater pool with a tanning shelf, spa waterfall, and lush landscape. Unwind in the hot tub or gather around the outdoor fire pit. The expansive outdoor space includes a built-in BBQ, wet bar, and serene courtyard for al fresco dining. Inside, enjoy a gourmet chef’s kitchen and cozy gas fireplace.
