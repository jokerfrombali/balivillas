About BaliVillas.ai

Born in Bali.

We're a team of Bali locals and technologists who got tired of watching travelers get lost in outdated listings — so we built something better.

Our story

It started with
one bad booking.

A villa that looked stunning online. Photos five years old, pool half-drained, Wi-Fi that died at sunset. Every traveler in Bali has this story — and so did we. The difference is, we live here, and we decided to fix it.

We spent a year on scooters, visiting villas one by one across Canggu, Uluwatu, Ubud, Seminyak and Sanur. We photographed them as they really are, tested the Wi-Fi, met the staff, checked the paperwork. Only then did a villa earn its place on our platform.

Then we added the technology: an AI search that understands plain human language. Tell it "quiet 2-bedroom near Berawa beach, fast Wi-Fi, under $1,500 a month" — and it finds your match in seconds, from villas we've personally verified.

Today BaliVillas.ai is both a platform and a hospitality team: AI on the surface, real people on the ground — housekeeping, maintenance, guest support, and transparent management for villa owners.
