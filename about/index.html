Our journey

It started with
one bad booking.

01

A year on scooters

A villa that looked stunning online turned out to be a half-drained pool with dying Wi-Fi. Every traveler has this story — we decided to end it. We spent a year visiting villas one by one across Canggu, Uluwatu, Ubud, Seminyak and Sanur: testing, photographing, meeting the staff, checking the paperwork. Only then did a villa earn its place on our platform.

02

Then we taught AI to speak villa

We built a search engine that understands plain human language. Tell it "quiet 2-bedroom near Berawa beach, fast Wi-Fi, under $1,500 a month" — and it finds your match in seconds. Not from scraped listings. Only from villas we verified ourselves.

03

Today: platform + full operations

BaliVillas.ai is now both a tech platform and a hospitality team. AI on the surface — housekeeping, pool and garden crews, guest support and transparent owner management on the ground. That's why owners trust us with their properties, and guests come back.
